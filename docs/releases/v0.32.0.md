# podcrates v0.32.0

The thirty-second release of podcrates. For podnet: the fix of
`relay connect` through SSH's `ProxyCommand` (T-327,
PodUtils/podnet#502) and the new crate `pod-link` (T-300). For podssh:
the integrity of TURN in `pod-proxy`'s `stun` (T-370, PodUtils/podssh#214)
and the services `smee-io` and `hostc` (T-329, PodUtils/podssh#213). For
podrunner: the parts of a `Template` (T-322).

## Crates

| Crate | Change | Consumers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `pod-relay` | the operator writes each frame of the node to its local side and flushes it, and the node flushes each session's stream: a local side with a buffer (a process's stdout) held sshd's binary KEXINIT, and SSH waited until sshd's `LoginGraceTime` (T-327, PodUtils/podnet#502). The live test `the_node_speaks_first_as_sshd_does` | podnet, podssh, podvm |
| `pod-link` | new: many streams over one byte stream of a carrier (`Link`, `Stream`), credit windows for each stream and for the link, a keepalive, the `Carrier` trait, and the unix and memory carriers (T-300, calls C120 to C124); a link over a resumable session of `pod-session` crosses the loss of its carrier (T-311) | podnet |
| `pod-proxy` | `stun`: `signed`, `integrity`, `fingerprinted`, `fingerprint_ok`, `long_term_key`, `message_type` and the methods and attributes of TURN (RFC 8656, RFC 6062), with the vectors of RFC 5769 (T-370) | podssh |
| `pod-services` | `smee-io` (kind `smee`) and `hostc` (kind `hostc`) (T-329); `serveo` skips `console.serveo.net` (`url-skip`); `localtunnel` holds its TLS name in place of RDAP (T-323) | podssh |
| `pod-services-watch` | each kind declares its arguments (`schema`), and a test and each run check each watch against them (T-324); `http-text` takes `url` or `endpoint`; `rdap` takes `ahead-days` (30; call C125) (T-323) | |
| `pod-broker` | the stage of the bind of a socket fits in the `sun_path` of macOS (T-325) | podnet, podrunner |
| `pod-actions-expr` | `Template::parts` and `TemplatePart` (T-322) | podrunner |

## How a consumer takes it

Each podcrates dependency names the tag `v0.32.0`.

- podnet: move the tag; `relay connect` through `ProxyCommand` works on a
  direct egress with no change of your glue. `pod-link` for your link
  workstream (PodUtils/podnet#480): `crates/pod-link/README.md` has the
  API, the parity with your #466 and #467, and how a link survives the
  loss of its carrier over `pod-session`.
- podssh: `pod_proxy::stun` for your TURN road (PodUtils/podssh@T-621);
  `pod_services::of_kind("smee")` and `get("hostc")` for your
  PodUtils/podssh@T-741 and PodUtils/podssh@T-742.
- podrunner: `Template::parts` for PodUtils/podrunner@T-089.
- podvm: move the tag.

## Breaking

- None in an API. `pod-services-watch` refuses a watch with a missing or
  an unknown argument before the network.

## Known defect

- `pod-netstack`: the reactor gives one ACK for the segments of one poll
  (T-197, open).
- `pod-session`: a random failure of one test under load (T-204, open).
- `pod-gsr` on Windows ARM64: `STATUS_ACCESS_VIOLATION` (T-131, open).

## Publish

`publish.yml` runs on the tag: the dry run of `cargo publish --workspace`.
The switch `crates-io` of `Cargo.toml` is false (the owner, 2026-11-09).
