# podcrates v0.28.0

The twenty-eighth release of podcrates. For podvm: the provision of a
guest writes its image with `qemu-img convert` (PodUtils/podvm#277), a
TCP list of `pod-confine` is refused on a target with `socketcall(2)`
(PodUtils/podvm#276), and `pod-services` lists the archives of workerd and
of wasmtime (PodUtils/podvm#274, #275). For podnet: the UDP of
`pod-netstack` (PodUtils/podnet#464), the IPv6 relay host and the plain
loopback wire of `pod-relay` (T-234), the watch of the relay contract
(PodUtils/podnet#463). For podssh: the TCP relays of Tox, the wss://
peers of Yggdrasil, the bootstrap nodes of HyperDHT (PodUtils/podssh#196,
#203, #204).

## Crates

| Crate | Change | Consumers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `pod-guest` | `convert_argv` in place of `commit_argv`: `qemu-img convert -f qcow2 -O qcow2 OVERLAY OUT` writes the chain of the overlay into a new image with no backing file; `qemu-img commit` has no output file and failed ("Expecting one image file name"). `provision` runs it. `tests/convert.rs` runs the `qemu-img` of the host (T-266) | podvm |

| `pod-netstack` | UDP (T-257 to T-259, calls C84 to C87): the reactor takes each datagram out of smoltcp at the packet that brings it, into a backlog of its socket (`udp_packets`, `udp_payload`); the next read of the app after a full channel wakes the reactor. `NetStats` counts `udp_rx_dropped`, `udp_tx_dropped` and `udp_oversize`. `StackConfig::max_datagram(to)`, `UdpSocket::max_datagram`, `UdpDatagrams::max_datagram` (IPv4 `mtu - 28`, IPv6 `mtu - 48`, each at most `udp_payload`); a send over it is `InvalidInput`. The feature `ipv4-fragmentation` (off by default) carries 65507 bytes to IPv4; with no feature an inbound IPv4 fragment is dropped and counted. A dropped UDP socket sends its queue, then frees its port. The gateway service goes on after a request over `GATEWAY_DATAGRAM` | podnet |
| `pod-relay` | a relay host as an IPv6 address in brackets (`[ADDR]`, `[ADDR]:PORT`; call C80); `open::open_on_loopback` (feature `plain-ws`, call C81): the forward opener on plain `ws://` to a stand-in relay on the loopback; with the feature, `parse_relay` takes `http://` and `ws://` for a host on the loopback (C82) (T-234) | podnet, podssh |
| `pod-services` | `tox-tcp-relay` (5 nodes on 443 with their keys; C83; T-244), `yggdrasil-wss-peer` (2 peers; T-256), `hyperdht-bootstrap` (3 nodes; T-260), `cloudflare-workerd-npm` (T-254), `wasmtime-release` (T-255); `ajam-relay` has the endpoint `contract` and the facts `contract-sha256` and `contract-bytes` (T-253); `node-trojan-70-34-207-14` is gone (T-174) | podssh, podvm, podnet |
| `pod-services-watch` | `http-json` takes the form `key` (T-244) | |

`live.yml` runs the live tests of the relay client of `pod-relay` by hand
(its job `relay`), the public mailboxes on Thursdays, and the tests of

## How a consumer takes it

Each podcrates dependency names the tag `v0.28.0`.

- podvm: rename `commit_argv` to `convert_argv` in the re-exports of
  `podvm_windows`, then run `kvm.yml` again (PodUtils/podvm#277). A profile
  with `net.ports` on i686 or powerpc64le now gets a refusal of the
  confine layer. `registry.npmjs.org` and the wasmtime release leave your
  list of reported hosts.
- podnet: your PodUtils/podnet@T-248 takes the UDP of `pod-netstack`
  (asks 3 to 5 of PodUtils/podnet#464). A literal of `NetStatsSnapshot`
  needs the three new fields or `..Default::default()`. The trial of
  `pod-relay` for podnet comes next (T-026).
- podssh: read `tox-tcp-relay`, `yggdrasil-wss-peer` and
  `hyperdht-bootstrap`; seven Trojan nodes stay (T-174).
- podrunner moves the tag. A TCP list on i686 is refused.

## Breaking

- `pod_guest::commit_argv` is `convert_argv`.
- `pod_confine`: a TCP list on a target with `socketcall(2)` is
  `Class::Unsupported`.
- `pod_netstack`: `NetStats` and `NetStatsSnapshot` have
  `udp_rx_dropped`, `udp_tx_dropped` and `udp_oversize`. A UDP send over
  `max_datagram` is `InvalidInput` (it was `Ok`, and lost).
  `UdpDatagrams::may_fragment` is false with no feature. With no feature,
  an inbound IPv4 fragment is dropped.
- `pod-services`: the service `node-trojan-70-34-207-14` is gone.

## Known defect

- `pod-confine`: on a target with `socketcall(2)` a TCP list and a closed
  UDP are refused: no rule of the filter sees a socket made through it.
- `pod-netstack`: the reactor gives one ACK for the segments of one poll
  (T-197, open).
- `pod-relay`: the `Host` of an HTTPS request to an IPv6 relay on port 443
  has no brackets (T-245, open); two relay hosts can have one name of a
  cache file (T-246, open).
- `pod-session`: a random failure of one test under load (T-204, open).
- `pod-gsr` on Windows ARM64: `STATUS_ACCESS_VIOLATION` (T-131, open).
- No watch reads the banner of an SSH forward relay yet (T-225, open).
