# podcrates v0.42.0

The forty-second release of podcrates. It carries the asks of each
consumer: podnet's of `pod-link`, `pod-hold` and `pod-wormhole` (#141),
podvm's overlay root and the schema of the probe report (#142), podssh's
of `pod-warp`, `pod-relay` and `pod-services`, and podrunner's container
engine.

## How a consumer takes it

Each podcrates dependency names the tag `v0.42.0`.

- podnet: `CarrierErrorKind::KeyRefused` in place of reading the close of
  the transport; `Calls::allow_raw()` for `--allow-raw icmp`;
  `Wormhole::derive_key("APPID/transit-key", 32)` for the transit key of
  a Python peer; `Config::words` for `--words N`; the feature `standin`
  of `pod-wormhole` for your stand-in mailbox.
- podssh: an arm for `Route::One` in the match of `route()` of your role
  (`crates/podssh-relay/src/serve/http_road/mod.rs`; T-563);
  `Road::tls(..).in_run(&ctx).with_forms(limits.http_forms)` and
  `PODSSH_HTTP_ROAD=one` for `Form::One`; the services `srv-us`,
  `tunnl-gg` and `serv00-piping`; the line of `pod-warp` on a key that is
  not the pinned one.
- podvm: `Root::Overlay` with `run_namespace_root`; `report::schema()` of
  `pod-hostprobe` with the feature `schema`.
- podrunner: `ContainerRoute::Engine` and `UnitNeed::container_engine`.

## Breaking

- `pod-wormhole`: `Code::make` takes the count of words.
- `pod-link`: `CarrierErrorKind` has the variant `KeyRefused`. A
  `match` with no wildcard names it.
- `pod-job`: `ContainerRoute` has the variant `Engine`, and `UnitNeed`
  the field `container_engine`. A struct literal names it.
- `pod-core`: `Dial` has the method `proxy_label`, with a default.
- `pod-relay`: `http_road::Route` has the variant `One`, which
  `http_road::route` gives for `/v1/http/one/`. podssh's role matches
  `route()` with no wildcard: it adds an arm for `Route::One`, which its
  own one form serves before `route()` (T-563).

## Known defect

- `pod-link`: the listener of the dns, icmp, udp and ws carriers gets no
  `KeyRefused` when the dialer refuses its key (T-557, open); an
  `arq-timeout` ends the whole link (T-514, open); the bytes of a poll
  listener outside `max_bytes` (T-525, open).
- `pod-hold`: the rule of raw sockets leaves `AF_PACKET` open (T-558,
  open).
- `pod-relay`: the one form runs over HTTP/1.1 only (T-560, blocked on
  podssh); the client of the HTTP road sends no `POST close` (T-478,
  open; the one form needs none).
- `pod-term`: no test compares `termios::Tty` with a kernel pty (T-540,
  open).
- `pod-gsr` on Windows ARM64: `STATUS_ACCESS_VIOLATION` (T-131, open).
