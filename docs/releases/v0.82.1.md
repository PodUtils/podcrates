# podcrates v0.82.1

A patch release of podcrates. `v0.82.0` has a tag but no run of
`release.yml`: it has no GitHub release, no signed `SHA256SUMS`, no assets
and no images. This release carries the content of `v0.82.0`
(`docs/releases/v0.82.0.md`) and each change of `main` after it, with the
assets, the images of `ghcr.io` and the hand-off (call C818). For podvm, it
carries the title and the dump flag of the init of a PID namespace of
`pod-enter`, the join of the payload of a run, and the meter of a cgroup of
`pod-hostprobe`. For podrunner, it carries the reasons of the absent times
of `run-report.v1`, the context data of the job message of
`pod-fake-github`, and the fixes of `pod-unit` and `pod-control`. For podnet,
it carries the retry budget of a registration of `pod-cloudflare`. For
podssh, it carries the fork of `iroh-relay` 1.4.2, the check of a secret
and the older marks of `pod-unit`, the checked replace of `pod-core`, and
the bind of a socket at a long path.

## Error texts

These lines change. A test that matches the old text gets the new one.

- `pod-cloudflare`: `no edge leg worked: LEG: CAUSE; ...` has the class TempFail when each leg failed with a refusal with retry: yes (was Unavailable). The event `tunnel.register`: `the edge refused the registration (CAUSE) and asks for a retry; the registration runs again in N ms`.
- `pod-core`, `Replace::write_checked`: `replace PATH: the check of the temporary file: CAUSE`.
- `pod-core`, `bind_staged`: `PATH: give a file name of 1 to 255 bytes, not N`, and `STAGE/s is longer than the N bytes of a socket address, and no short path to it was made (/proc/self/fd, a link in /tmp or /var/tmp): CAUSE`.
- `pod-unit`, a unit of the system with `User=NAME`: `# Relaxed, as the account NAME can be a person's, whose IPC a stop would remove: RemoveIPC=yes`.
- `pod-job`, `RunReport::check`: a key of `unknown_reasons` that names a present field is refused with `which is not an unknown measured field or an absent time`.

## Breaking changes

- `pod_enter::Plan` has the field `relay_title`. A struct literal of `Plan` needs it (`None` keeps the product of `Plan::names`).
- `pod_enter`: the init of a PID namespace is not dumpable, also in a user namespace of the rung (T-1113). A caller with no `CAP_SYS_PTRACE` over the user namespace of the caller of the run cannot open `/proc/PID/ns/*` or `/proc/PID/root` of the init, or `setns` on its pidfd. Call `pod_enter::joinable(pidfd of the init)`; with a `Member`, read `/proc/PID` of `Member::pid` and join `Member::pidfd`; `None` keeps the init.
- `pod_unit`: a unit of the system with `User=NAME` keeps the IPC of the account at its stop (`RemoveIPC=yes` is a comment), unless the program calls `unit.hardening.service_account()` for its own account (T-1107). A unit that restarts after each end has `StartLimitInterval=0`: a golden file of a unit gets the two lines (T-1108).
- `pod_unit::Unit::check` refuses an older mark of fewer than 8 characters or with a control character (T-1106).
- `pod_cloudflare`: the serial limit of a leg is 110 s for `quic` and 210 s for `http2` and `http2+relay` (was 130 s and 280 s), and "no edge leg worked" can have the class TempFail (exit 75). `pod_cloudflare::standin::Reply` is `non_exhaustive`: an exhaustive `match` gets a `_` arm.
- `pod_control`: `Settings::write_timeout` is the wait for one whole line, not for each write of the system. A call back ends as `CallFault::Gone` at `write_timeout + read_timeout` from its start.
- `pod_iroh` takes `iroh` and `iroh-dns` `>=1.4.2, <1.5`. A consumer with its own `iroh` below 1.4.2 moves to 1.4.2.
- `pod_services::get` gives `None` for `node-trojan-66-163-112-121`, `node-trojan-169-197-140-218` and `node-trojan-216-238-104-154`, and `syncthing-relay` has no relay DVWOU27.
- The error texts of the section "Error texts".
