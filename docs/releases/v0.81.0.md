# podcrates v0.81.0

The eighty-first release of podcrates. For podvm, it carries the bound of
the connect of the QMP monitor of `pod-guest` (so podvm can take
`pod_guest::warm::line`), the static QEMU 11.1.3 of PodUtils/static-pkgs,
which saves an NVMe disk, the streamed read of a log of `pod-supervise` and
the read of a file in pieces of `pod-incus`. For podrunner and the CI of
githome, it carries the wipe of each copy of a control line of
`pod-control` and the install of a unit of the system of `pod-unit`. For
each consumer, it carries the one atomic replace of a file at a path of
`pod-core` (`replace`, `Replace`).

## Error texts

These lines change. A test that matches the old text gets the new one.

- `pod-guest`, `qmp::Monitor::connect` (T-1056): `the QMP socket PATH never appeared within N.Ns` (the seconds have one decimal; before `Ns`); new: `the queue of the QMP socket PATH is full for N.Ns: the emulator serves one client at a time and queues one more, so other connections to it are open; close them before a new one`, `the QMP socket PATH refused each connection for N.Ns: no emulator listens on it`, `qmp connect to PATH: no connection within N.Ns: CAUSE`, and for a greeting that does not come `qmp: no greeting from PATH within N.Ns: the emulator serves one client at a time, so this connection waits behind another open connection, or the emulator holds its lock`.
- `pod-core`, `files::replace` and `Replace::write`: `replace PATH: STEP: CAUSE` (new functions; `replace_private` keeps the error of the system).
- `pod-incus`, `ErrorKind::Output`: `CALL: the write of the output: CAUSE`.
- `pod-unit`, `systemd::render` of a unit of the system: `the account "NAME": give 1 to 32 of A-Z a-z 0-9 _ . -, not first a -` (`Class::Usage`).
- `pod-supervise`: a symbolic link at the place of a log is an error of the open (`ELOOP`) for `logs` and `follow_tail`, as for `follow`.

## Pins

- The static QEMU of podvm's store: `pod-services`, the service `static-pkgs-qemu`, gives QEMU 11.1.3 of the release `qemu-11.1.3` of `github.com/PodUtils/static-pkgs` (built from the upstream tag `v11.1.3`, commit `005c61c0`): the amd64 archives `qemu-system-x86_64-11.1.3-linux-amd64.tar.gz`, `qemu-system-aarch64-11.1.3-linux-amd64.tar.gz`, `qemu-img-11.1.3-linux-amd64.tar.gz` and `qemu-data-11.1.3-linux-amd64.tar.gz` (`share/qemu`), each with its SHA-256 and length, and each binary with its member, SHA-256 and length. It holds the QEMU commits `9d378cab` (the migration of an NVMe device, so the plan line saves its NVMe root disk) and `2865bf1c` (the fix of the use-after-free with dispatch, PodUtils/podvm#618). Each pinned archive has an attestation of `PodUtils/static-pkgs/.github/workflows/release.yml`, and each binary is static. `services.yml` 44806797116 fetched each archive at its SHA-256 and length; `guest.yml` 44809029300 saved the plan line with its NVMe root disk on it and started it warm. The service `hermeticbuild-qemu` is removed.

## Breaking changes

- `pod_control::wire::Request` implements `Drop` (it wipes its params). A field cannot move out of a `Request`: `serde_json::from_value(r.params)` is error E0509. Take the field with `std::mem::take(&mut r.params)`, or read it by reference (`T::deserialize(&r.params)`). podrunner: `parse` of `crates/podrunner/src/control.rs` (the method `drain`).
- `pod_control::wire::request_line` and `wire::answer_line` give `Zeroizing<String>`, not `String`. It derefs to `String` (`line.as_bytes()` and `&line` work), but it has no `Display`: `format!("{line}")` is error E0277; write `line.as_str()`. A caller that needs a `String` copies it, and the copy is not wiped. podrunner: `"{line}"` in `crates/podrunner/src/control_tests.rs` (`each_request_reads_back_to_itself`, the assert of `parse(line.as_bytes())`).
- `pod_unit::install::Form` has the variant `Service`. An exhaustive `match` of a consumer gets an arm. No consumer matches `Form`.
- `pod_unit::systemd::render` refuses an account in `User=` that is not a plain name (section "Error texts").
- `pod_services`: the service `hermeticbuild-qemu` is removed; `static-pkgs-qemu` has the same facts, and also `nvme-commit`. podvm: `pod_services::get("hermeticbuild-qemu")` in `crates/podvm/src/hosts_tests.rs` (`the_store_copy_of_qemu_holds_the_facts_of_pod_services`) gives `None`, so the test fails until it names `static-pkgs-qemu` and `crates/podvm-windows/src/tools.rs` holds the pins of 11.1.3.
- `pod_guest::qmp::Monitor::connect`: a connect to a full queue fails at once and is tried again up to `wait`, where it waited in the kernel with no bound. The `Timeout` texts change (section "Error texts").
- `pod_supervise`: `logs` and `follow_tail` refuse a symbolic link at the place of the log, as `follow` does.
- The error texts of the section "Error texts".

The new enums are `pod_core::files::Mode`, `pod_core::files::Step` (`non_exhaustive`) and `pod_supervise::stream::Fault`. `pod_incus::ErrorKind` (`non_exhaustive`) has the variant `Output`.

- `scripts/check-private-replace.py`: `pod_core::files::replace` is the one replace of a file.

- The proofs of the nightly run 44792996234 at `v0.80.0`: on macOS the timeout of a closed Unix socket (T-1041) and the node and npm legs of `tls_clients` (T-1042); on Windows the git tests of `pod-actions-store` (T-1030) and the doc test of `pod_control::conformance` (T-1040).
