# podcrates v0.31.0

The thirty-first release of podcrates. For podvm: two defects of its
arm64 work (PodUtils/podvm#281, PodUtils/podvm#282). For podrunner: a
`Run` of `pod-actions-workflow` that owns its workflow (T-309). For
podnet: the display form of a fingerprint (T-278).

## Crates

| Crate | Change | Consumers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `pod-extract` | the hash of a layer reads the rest of the blob after the end marker of tar (`HashedStream::finish`): the padding of a GNU tar to its record of 10 240 bytes is no mismatch (T-320, PodUtils/podvm#281) | podvm |
| `pod-enter` | `abi::libc_beside`: the search of the C library looks at the multiarch places first (the name in a library root and one level under it), and walks each real root once, breadth-first: the aarch64 glibc of a merged-usr Debian image is found (T-321, PodUtils/podvm#282) | podvm |
| `pod-actions-workflow` | `Run<W: Borrow<Workflow>>`: a run owns its workflow (`Workflow`, `Arc<Workflow>`) or borrows it; `Run::workflow`; `ready` gives owned ids (T-309) | podrunner |
| `pod-identity` | `DisplayFingerprint` (`of`, `parse`, `matches`) and `PublicKey::display_fingerprint`: five groups of eight characters of base32 with a checksum of 4 bytes (T-278, call C118) | podnet |

## How a consumer takes it

Each podcrates dependency names the tag `v0.31.0`.

- podvm: move the tag; `podvm import` of a GNU tar and the loader family
  on a Debian or Ubuntu image on arm64 work (experiment 356).
- podrunner: a `Run` over `Workflow` or `Arc<Workflow>` replaces the
  `Box::leak` of its `LocalSource`; pass `&id` to `expand` and `finish`.
- podnet: `DisplayFingerprint` for its T-261.
- podssh: move the tag.

## Breaking

- `pod_actions_workflow::Run` takes a type parameter (`Run<&'w Workflow>`
  for a borrow), and `Run::ready` gives `Vec<String>`.

## Known defect

- `pod-netstack`: the reactor gives one ACK for the segments of one poll
  (T-197, open).
- `pod-session`: a random failure of one test under load (T-204, open).
- `pod-gsr` on Windows ARM64: `STATUS_ACCESS_VIOLATION` (T-131, open).

## Publish

`publish.yml` runs on the tag: the dry run of `cargo publish --workspace`.
The switch `crates-io` of `Cargo.toml` is false (the owner, 2026-11-09).
