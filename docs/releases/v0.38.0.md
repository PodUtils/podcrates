# podcrates v0.38.0

The thirty-eighth release of podcrates. For podnet (its complete beta,
#133): the port rules of `pod-warp` (T-475), the TXT and CNAME reader of
`pod-dns` (T-305), the exporter and the record layer of `pod-e2e`
(T-301), the accept loop of `pod-ws` (T-439) and the carriers `ws` and
`h2` of `pod-link` (T-420, T-421). For podvm: a bind of `/dev/pts` in
the namespace rung of `pod-enter` (T-504) and the knob flags of
`pod-netlab` (T-503). For podrunner: the actor classes `agent` and `bot`
of `pod-job` (T-505). For each consumer: the tests build on Windows
(T-488), and three tests of `pod-services-watch` take the ways of
Windows (T-507).

## Crates

| Crate | Change | Consumers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `pod-warp` | the rules `port:N=WAY`, `port:N-M=WAY` and `tcp:HOST:PORT=WAY` (a name, an IPv4 address or a bracketed IPv6 address); they match a TCP CONNECT only; the decision functions of `Table` (`decide`, `decide_static`, `for_name`, `for_ip`, `for_addresses`) and `RuleMatch::matches` take the port of the target (T-475, call C247) | podnet, podssh |
| `pod-dns` | the module `txt`: `query` with the caller's ID and an EDNS0 size; `read` gives the TXT strings, the CNAME targets, the TC bit, the full RCODE and the server's EDNS0 size; a cut answer is taken only with TC; no transport (T-305, call C248) | podnet |
| `pod-e2e` | `Channel::{handshake_hash, exporter, agree, into_transport}`; the exporter refuses the labels `pod-e2e ...` of the crate; `record::Records`: the header of 24 bytes as the associated data, the suites `chacha20-poly1305` and `aes-256-gcm` agreed after the verdict (`Error::NoSuite`), a rekey at 2^31 records, 1 GiB or 1 hour, the padding to buckets, a replay window of 1024 counters, a single-use RESUME (T-301, calls C246, C255 to C258) | podnet |
| `pod-ws` | feature `serve`: `pod_ws::serve`, the accept loop with a deadline for each stage, caps for each source, eviction of the oldest connection of the earliest stage, `TCP_NODELAY` and `TCP_NOTSENT_LOWAT` (T-439, calls C250 to C252) | podnet, podssh |
| `pod-link` | feature `ws`: the WebSocket carrier, `KEY@wss://HOST[:PORT]/PATH`, with the pin of an Ed25519 key, its listener on `pod_ws::serve`; feature `h2`: one POST of HTTP/2 with the same pin (T-420, T-421, calls C253, C254) | podnet |
| `pod-enter` | the namespace rung binds the host's `/dev/pts` on the `/dev/pts` of the image where that directory exists; a refusal writes one line and the rung goes on; the module `ns_optional` holds the proc mount and this bind (`ns_pts_target`, `ns_refused_line`, `NS_PTS_STEP`) (T-504) | podvm |
| `pod-netlab` | `Knobs::flags` writes no empty `--tcp` or `--udp` (`none` is the empty list); a number past its type names the range of the knob; a bad word lists the words of the flag (`WORDS` of each enum); `KnobError::BadFlag` carries `why: String` (T-503) | podvm |
| `pod-job` | `ActorClass::{Agent, Bot}` (`agent`, `bot`), in `job-offer.v1` too (T-505) | podrunner |
| `pod-hostprobe` | `sys::MS_BIND` | podvm |

`scripts/agent/check-windows.sh` checks the tests of the workspace for

## How a consumer takes it

Each podcrates dependency names the tag `v0.38.0`.

- podnet: change your `shadow.rs` for the new rule kinds of `pod-warp`.
  `pod_dns::txt`, `pod_e2e::record` and the `ws` and `h2` carriers of
  `pod-link` are there for your T-278, T-281, T-298 to T-301, T-269.
- podssh: move the tag. Your relay role (T-036) can take
  `pod_ws::serve`.
- podvm: move the tag. `run -t` on the namespace rung gets `/dev/pts`
  (your T-1760, PodUtils/podvm#296); `podvm lab` prints flags that read
  back (PodUtils/podvm#298).
- podrunner: move the tag. An offer with `actor_class` `agent` or `bot`
  parses (PodUtils/podrunner#80).

## Breaking

- `pod-warp`: the decision functions of `Table` and `RuleMatch::matches`
  take a port argument.
- `pod-netlab`: `KnobError::BadFlag::why` is a `String`.
- `pod-job`: `ActorClass` has two more variants; a `match` with no
  wildcard needs arms for them.
- `pod-e2e`: `Error::NoSuite` is new; podssh's `judged` in
  `crates/podssh-cli/src/channel.rs` takes it beside `Handshake` (the
  trial patch `adoption/podssh/pod-warp.patch` has the line).
- podnet's `tests/rule_words.rs` calls `matcher.matches(host, ip, None)`
  (the trial patch `adoption/podnet/pod-warp.patch` has the line).

## Known defect

- `pod-link`: the ws carrier does not follow podnet's rules of its
  WebSocket carrier yet (the subprotocol, a text frame, Ping and Pong;
  T-506, in work).
- `pod-relay`: the client of the HTTP road sends no `POST close` when the
  app drops its stream (T-478, open).
- `pod-netstack`: a stream dropped before its handshake ends finishes the
  handshake later (T-482, open); the reactor gives one ACK for the
  segments of one poll (T-197, open).
- `pod-session`: a random failure of one test under load (T-204, open).
- `pod-gsr` on Windows ARM64: `STATUS_ACCESS_VIOLATION` (T-131, open).

## Publish

`publish.yml` runs on the tag: the dry run of `cargo publish --workspace`.
The switch `crates-io` of `Cargo.toml` is false (the owner, 2026-11-09).
`pod-link`, `pod-turn`, `pod-devtunnel`, `pod-warp` and `pod-netlab` are
not reserved on crates.io yet: the owner reserves them before a publish.
