# podcrates v0.36.0

The thirty-sixth release of podcrates. For podssh: the invariants 6 to 10
of the HTTP road (T-476, PodUtils/podssh#221) and the end of the deadlock
of its serial form (T-477, PodUtils/podssh#222). For podvm: the report of
`pod-hostprobe` with its reader (T-485, PodUtils/podvm#291). For podnet:
the adoption trial of `pod-warp` (T-412).

## Crates

| Crate | Change | Consumers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `pod-relay` | the HTTP road: a stored `seq` sent again gets the same acknowledgement, an acknowledgement goes only after the chunk is stored, a refusal names its reason (`full`, `unknown session`, `not allowed`), the close of a direction carries its count, and each resend carries the same (session, `seq`) (T-476, calls C230 to C232); the new module `http_road::window`, the receiver of the role's server, with no I/O; the serial form writes the bytes down from a task of its own through a channel of 4 answers, and while it is full each batch up goes alone, so an app whose writer waits for its reader (SSH) does not stop the session (T-477, calls C233 to C235) | podssh |
| `pod-hostprobe` | feature `report`: `report::write(&Findings, &Selection)` and `report::read(document)`, a versioned JSON object of the crate's own types (`report::SCHEMA` 1); `read` refuses an unknown version, a row that `probes::PROBES` does not name, a duplicate row, an unknown word and a denial with no errno (T-485, calls C241 to C243); `Rung::requirement()` | podvm |

`adoption/podnet/pod-warp.patch` (T-412) is the trial of `pod-warp` in
podnet at its `9c1fc4b`: it passes each of its 23 steps here and on
GitHub (adoption run 43347062071).

## How a consumer takes it

Each podcrates dependency names the tag `v0.36.0`.

- podssh: the server of your relay role sends `X-Seq` on the answer that
  stores a batch, `X-Refuse` with its reason on a refusal, `total=` beside
  `fin=1`, and `X-Total` on the `410` of `1000`, and takes
  `pod_relay::http_road::window::Up` for the batches up (the README of
  `pod-relay`, "The HTTP road"). Turn your ignored test of the serial form
  on again.
- podvm: `pod-hostprobe` with `features = ["report"]`: embed
  `report::write` under one key of your `report::document`, and read it
  back from your cache with `report::read` (your T-1746).
- podnet: move the tag. The trial patch of `pod-warp` is on `main`; the
  COORD issue of the freeze of your `src/warp/` follows the trial of
  podssh (T-413).
- podrunner: move the tag.

## Breaking

- `pod-relay`: the wire of the HTTP road. The client requires `X-Seq` on
  each stored batch and `X-Total` on a close of `1000`: a server with no
  such headers fails each session. Take the client and the server
  together.

## Known defect

- `pod-relay`: the client of the HTTP road sends no `POST close` when the
  app drops its stream (T-478, open).
- `pod-netstack`: the reactor gives one ACK for the segments of one poll
  (T-197, open).
- `pod-session`: a random failure of one test under load (T-204, open).
- `pod-gsr` on Windows ARM64: `STATUS_ACCESS_VIOLATION` (T-131, open).

## Publish

`publish.yml` runs on the tag: the dry run of `cargo publish --workspace`.
The switch `crates-io` of `Cargo.toml` is false (the owner, 2026-11-09).
`pod-link`, `pod-turn`, `pod-devtunnel`, `pod-warp` and `pod-netlab` are
not reserved on crates.io yet: the owner reserves them before a publish.
