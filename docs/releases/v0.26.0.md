# podcrates v0.26.0

The twenty-sixth release of podcrates. For podnet: the veth wire of the
netns hold in `pod-hold` (a namespace with no `/dev/net/tun`), and the
facts of the SSH forward relays in `pod-services` with a reader of a
relay's banner. For podrunner: the time to the first step, the job images
and the exit reason `limit` in a report of `pod-job`.

## Crates

| Crate | Change | Consumers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `pod-hold` | the veth wire of the netns hold, from podnet's `netns/veth.rs` at `db34bae`: `Wire { Tun, Veth }` (`of(fd)`, `checks_checksums()`, `read_buffer(mtu)`, `word()`), `WireChoice { Auto, Tun, Veth }` (in place of podnet's process variable), `open_wire`, `create_veth`, `take_frame`, `Frame`, `frame_for`, `GUEST_MAC`, `GATEWAY_MAC`, `WIRE_NAME`, `ETH_HEADER`. The smoltcp device stays in podnet. The netns tests run as root in CI and fail on a skip (T-201) | podnet |
| `pod-services` | the facts of the SSH forward relays (`pinggy`, `serveo`, `localhost-run`, `sandhole`): `user`, `url-match`, `url-skip`, `keepalive-seconds`, and the order of the plain SSH endpoints; `dashboard-host` of `pinggy` moves to `url-skip`. `forward::Relay::of(&Service)` (`endpoints`, `user`, `url_rule`, `session_limit`, `keepalive`), `forward::UrlRule` (a URL holds each text of `url-match` and no text of `url-skip`), `forward::Banner` (holds a word that two chunks cut; a bound of 2048 bytes; it reads a URL after a color escape) (T-224, call C66) | podnet, podssh |
| `pod-job` | `ExitReason::Limit` (the unit ended the job over a bound of its limits); `RunReport::claim_to_first_step_s` and `RunReport::job_images` (private: the public report leaves them out; `check` refuses a job image that is not `sha256:` and 64 hex digits). The schemas of `schemas/` hold them (T-226) | podrunner |

## How a consumer takes it

Each podcrates dependency names the tag `v0.26.0`.

- podnet applies `adoption/podnet/pod-hold-veth.patch` (its trial at
  podnet `db34bae`): `netns/veth.rs` becomes glue over the crate and
  `netns/tun.rs` uses `pod_hold::netns::Wire`. Its `UrlRule` of
  `ssh-relay` reads `url-skip` of `pinggy` (it read `dashboard-host`), or
  takes `pod_services::forward`.
- podssh: its reader of Pinggy (`pinggy::dashboard()`) reads `url-skip`
  of `pinggy` (it read `dashboard-host`).
- podrunner sets the two fields and the reason in its reports (its T-075).
- podvm moves the tag; no API of its own changes.

## Breaking

- `pod_hold::Hold::netns(pid, userns, wire, name)`: the wire is an
  argument, so the proof of a veth hold names the veth wire.
- `pod_services`: the fact `dashboard-host` of `pinggy` is now the first
  text of `url-skip`.
- `pod_job::RunReport` has the fields `claim_to_first_step_s` and
  `job_images`; `ExitReason` has `Limit`.

## Known defect

- `pod-confine`: SCTP, SMC, Unix sockets with a path and the UDP rights of
  a newer Landlock are not covered under a TCP list or a closed UDP
  (T-213, open).
- `pod-netstack`: the reactor gives one ACK for the segments of one poll
  (T-197, open); a SYN sent again can reach a newer listen socket of the
  pool of a listener (T-223, open).
- `pod-session`: a random failure of one test under load (T-204, open).
- `pod-gsr` on Windows ARM64: `STATUS_ACCESS_VIOLATION` (T-131, open).
- No watch reads the banner of an SSH forward relay yet (T-225, open).

## Publish

`publish.yml` runs on the tag: the dry run of `cargo publish --workspace`.
The switch `crates-io` of `Cargo.toml` is false (the owner, 2026-11-09).
