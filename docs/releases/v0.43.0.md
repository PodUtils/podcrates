# podcrates v0.43.0

The forty-third release of podcrates. It carries podnet's preshared key
and responder of `pod-wg` (#144), and podrunner's two new crates of the
actions (PodUtils/podrunner#94, #95).

## Crates

| Crate | Change | Consumers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `pod-wg` | `Keys::preshared: Option<[u8; 32]>` (`None`: the zero key), mixed in at the psk step of the handshake; the stand-in's `respond_preshared` and `start_preshared` (T-561, PodUtils/podnet#547, call C314) | podnet |
| `pod-wg` | `Responder`, `Peer` and `Incoming`: a responder sans I/O for a list of peers, with cookies under load and a rekey that keeps the old session until the peer sends in the new one; the constants `REJECT_AFTER`, `KEEPALIVE_TIMEOUT`, `COOKIE_SECRET_AGE`, `INITIATION_GAP`, `REJECT_AFTER_MESSAGES`; nine new `Error` variants (T-562, PodUtils/podnet#547, calls C315 to C319) | podnet |
| `pod-actions-store` | New. The actions by tag: the sorted lock `OWNER/REPO@REF COMMIT sha256:HEX` (`lock::Lock`, `Pin`), `store::resolve` through each composite action, `workflow_uses`, the `Forge` trait and its `Git` forge, `Mode` (`Online`, `Frozen`, `Offline`), the digest of the uncompressed tar (T-564, PodUtils/podrunner#94, calls C320 to C324) | podrunner |
| `pod-actions-results` | New. The `ArtifactService` of the results API and its blob routes: `ArtifactStore` (one store for each run, with a limit), `Answer`, `Payload`, `SERVICE`, `BLOBS`, `LIMIT`, `token::{backend_ids, results_scope}` (T-565, PodUtils/podrunner#95, calls C325 to C329) | podrunner |

## How a consumer takes it

Each podcrates dependency names the tag `v0.43.0`.

- podnet: `preshared` in each `pod_wg::Keys` literal (`None` keeps the
  current handshake); `PresharedKey` of a WireGuard file into it;
  `Responder` for a WireGuard server of several peers.
- podrunner: `pod-actions-store` in place of its `lock.rs` and
  `store.rs` (`LOCK_FILE` stays in podrunner); `pod-actions-results` in
  place of its ArtifactService.
- podssh and podvm: no change of their crates; the bump of the tag only.

## Breaking

- `pod-wg`: `Keys` has the field `preshared`. A struct literal names it
  (T-571: the adoption trial of podnet sets `preshared: None`).
- `pod-wg`: `Error` has nine new variants. A `match` with no wildcard
  names them.

## Known defect

- `pod-actions-results`: no fuzz target reads the input of a stranger
  (T-566, open).
- `pod-link`: the listener of the dns, icmp, udp and ws carriers gets no
  `KeyRefused` when the dialer refuses its key (T-557, open); an
  `arq-timeout` ends the whole link (T-514, open); the bytes of a poll
  listener outside `max_bytes` (T-525, open).
- `pod-hold`: the rule of raw sockets leaves `AF_PACKET` open (T-558,
  open).
- `pod-relay`: the one form runs over HTTP/1.1 only (T-560, blocked on
  podssh); the client of the HTTP road sends no `POST close` (T-478,
  open).
- `pod-term`: no test compares `termios::Tty` with a kernel pty (T-540,
  open).
- `pod-gsr` on Windows ARM64: `STATUS_ACCESS_VIOLATION` (T-131, open).

## Publish

`publish.yml` runs on the tag: the dry run of `cargo publish --workspace`.
The switch `crates-io` of `Cargo.toml` is false (the owner, 2026-11-09).
The owner reserves `pod-actions-store` and `pod-actions-results` on
crates.io before a publish ("Owner actions" of `TODO/PROGRESS.md`).
