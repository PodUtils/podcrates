# podcrates v0.83.0

The eighty-third release of podcrates. It carries each change of `main`
after `v0.82.1`. It has one new crate, `pod-natprobe`, and the feature
`transfer` of `pod-term`. For podnet, it carries the failures of the QA
pass of `pod-ws`, `pod-proxy`, `pod-dns`, `pod-tls`, `pod-warp`,
`pod-cloudflare`, `pod-wormhole`, `pod-hold` and `pod-unit` (each stage and
each cause once), and the probe of a Unix socket of `pod-core`. For podvm,
it carries the bounds, the refusals by size and the exec codes of
`pod-incus`, `usage::Mounts` of `pod-hostprobe`, and the sparse files of
`pod-extract`. For podssh, it carries the DNS carrier of `pod-link`, the
modes and the transfer codec of `pod-term`, the bound of a unit name, and
the classes `CantCreate` and `Internal`. For podrunner, it carries the fork
fact of `pod-fake-githome`.

## New crate

| Crate | What | Consumers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `pod-natprobe` | A probe of the NAT on the path of a host (T-802, GitHub #212). The core has no I/O (`engine::ProbeMachine`): the mapping and the filtering of RFC 5780 on separate sockets with positive controls, the port allocation, the IP pooling, hairpinning, CGNAT, NAT64, and the gateway (NAT-PMP, PCP, UPnP IGD, with no change of a mapping). `Prober` runs it on tokio; with `default-features = false` the crate builds with no tokio. The reply of a server is untrusted: a URL of UPnP is the gateway's IP alone (`wire::ssdp::gateway_url`), an OTHER-ADDRESS has the class of the server's address, a hairpin datagram goes to a public or shared address only, and the report keeps at most 256 observations of dropped datagrams (`Report::dropped_unrecorded`). `resolve_pod_services_stun(catalog, resolver)` takes the servers of the kind `stun` of `pod-services`. A live test runs in `live.yml` (T-802, T-1151 to T-1154) | podnet, podssh |

## Breaking changes

- `pod_wormhole::Failed::Unreachable` holds `Missed`, not `String`. A caller that binds the field as a `String` adds `.to_string()` (`Missed` has `Display` with the same words). `Failed::Unreachable(_)` builds as it is.
- `pod_core::Class` has two new variants, `CantCreate` (73) and `Internal` (70), and `Class::ALL` has 15 items. An exhaustive `match` on `Class` needs two arms. podssh's `code_of` (`devtunnel.rs`, no `_` arm) does not build until it adds both; a `match` of podnet with a `_` arm builds.
- `pod_release::Error::CantWrite` has the class `CantCreate` and the exit code 73.
- `pod_warp::provider::EnrolError` has the new variant `NoAnswer(PathFailure)`, which a failed path to the API now gives in place of `Unreachable(String)`. An exhaustive `match` adds an arm, and a match of `Unreachable` for this case matches `NoAnswer` too. An answer that does not decode is `EnrolError::Protocol`.
- `pod_warp::find::answers` is removed. Call `pod_core::files::socket_answers`. podnet does not call `answers`; podnet removes its own `socket_answers` and the rule in `claim_unix_path` at this tag.
- New public fields of public structs. A struct literal that names each field adds them: `pod_dns::txt::Answer::authoritative`, `pod_hostprobe::sys::Statfs::f_bfree` (the struct has `Default`), `pod_incus::Settings::answer_timeout` (podvm uses `Settings::new`), `pod_extract::pax::Member::sparse` (`Option<Sparse>`; podvm builds no `Member`), `pod_link::Dns::handshake_quiet`. A caller whose dialers set `DnsSettings::poll_max` above 5 s sets `handshake_quiet` above that on its listeners.
- `pod_link` `DnsSettings` has the new public field `hold`, and `DnsSettingsError` the new variant `Hold`: a struct literal with no `..Default::default()` adds the field, and an exhaustive match adds the variant. A caller with a `query_timeout` of 1 s or less lowers `hold` (T-1166).
- `pod_incus`: an answer over `Settings::max_body` is `ErrorKind::TooLarge`, not `ErrorKind::Io` with the words of `pod-proxy`. `ErrorKind` is `non_exhaustive`. podvm's class of a refusal by size reads the words of an `Io` error and finds none (T-1143): match `ErrorKind::TooLarge`.
- `pod_incus`: an exec that ends with 126 or 127 gives its code from `exec_recorded` and `Attached::wait`, not an `Incus` error with the status 400. podvm removes its read of the operation after the wait, its `code_of_failure` and its bound of 30 s on the first `GET /1.0` (T-1158); it keeps its guard of SIGPIPE.
- `pod_extract`: a GNU sparse file of PAX extracts with its holes; it did not extract before (the layer was refused).
- `pod_services`: the kind `stun` has three default servers. The doctor of podssh takes each server by rank, and its test `the_variable_names_the_servers_or_turns_the_line_off` pins two. `pod_services::get` gives `None` for `node-trojan-45-63-30-142`.
- `pod_testkit` depends on `libc` for each Unix target.
- The error texts and the exit codes of the sections above.
