# podcrates v0.75.0

The seventy-fifth release of podcrates. It carries the asks of podrunner
first (the ruling "podrunner first"): each member of a compressed layer for
`pod-extract`, and the first images of podcrates on ghcr.io (the ruling
"ghcr.io", call C789). It also carries the move of the layer under a small
cap of a relay for podssh, and the updates of Dependabot.

## Crates

| Crate | Change | Consumers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `pod-extract` | A layer of more than one gzip member (eStargz, bgzip, a concatenation) or of more than one zstd frame is read whole: before, the decoder stopped after the first member, and the archive was cut short with no error. After a member, the next one starts only when the bytes that follow begin with its magic; bytes after the last member stay for the hash of the blob. A zstd skippable frame is skipped (T-956, PodUtils/podrunner#317 ask 2) | podrunner, podvm |
| `pod-session` | While a move is in progress, the client holds the old link: it takes no new bytes of the application, sends no new `ACK` and no queued `DATA` on it, and answers each `PING` with the offset acknowledged before; the new link carries the rest. A failed move lifts the hold at once. The wire does not change (T-958, PodUtils/podssh#325, call C790) | podssh, podnet |
| `pod-relay` | `forward_layer::offer::window_under(cap)`, and so `Offer::window` and `Offer::fit`, give an eighth of the cap, not a quarter: with the move at three quarters, the old link ends under seven eighths of the cap (T-958) | podssh |

## Release assets

`podlab`, `pod-fake-github` and `pod-fake-githome` for
`x86_64-unknown-linux-musl` and `aarch64-unknown-linux-musl`, with a line
of each in the signed `SHA256SUMS`. New in this release: each of the three
is an image too, at `ghcr.io/podutils/NAME:v0.75.0` (`linux/amd64` and
`linux/arm64`, `FROM scratch`, the binary at `/usr/local/bin/NAME`), with
its provenance attested in the registry. The body of the GitHub release has
the digest of each index and of each platform beside the SHA-256 of each
asset (T-954, call C789).

## How a consumer takes it

Each podcrates dependency names the tag `v0.75.0`; `issue-sync.yml@v0.75.0` takes `podcrates_ref: v0.75.0`.

- podrunner and podvm: a layer of many gzip members or zstd frames now extracts whole; no change of code.
- podrunner: pin `pod-fake-githome` and `pod-fake-github` by the digest of their index on ghcr.io, beside their line of `SHA256SUMS`.
- podssh: no change of code; the role's end and the client take the window of an eighth of the cap from `window_under` and `Offer::fit`. Check in your `layer_cap.rs` that no link ends with 1009.
- podnet: the tag only.
