# podcrates v0.74.0

The seventy-fourth release of podcrates. It carries the asks of podrunner
first (the ruling "podrunner first"): a runner job through podrunner's
`serve` from the fake of githome's source to the broker of the fake
GitHub, and test 15 of podrunner's `source-conformance`. It also carries
the window of the layer under a small cap of a relay for podssh, and the
fixes of the red nightly run.

## Crates

| Crate | Change | Consumers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `pod-testkit` | `pod-fake-githome serve --github URL`: each job queued in the fake of githome is queued in the `pod-fake-github` at `URL` too, and `mint` of a claimed job acts as the App: it mints a JIT runner with the runner name of the claim and the labels of the offer, gives the job to that runner, and answers the files of `encoded_jit_config`. With no `--github`, `mint` gives the made-up files as before. The feature `githome` takes the feature `github`; `pod_testkit::github::client` is the App side (`installation_token`, `jit_runner`) (T-952, PodUtils/podrunner#311, call C788) | podrunner |

## How a consumer takes it

Each podcrates dependency names the tag `v0.74.0`; `issue-sync.yml@v0.74.0` takes `podcrates_ref: v0.74.0`.

- podrunner: run `pod-fake-github serve`, then `pod-fake-githome serve --github URL` with the base URL of the first; name that URL in `[egress]`. Queue the job on githome (`POST /_fake/jobs`, with `steps` or the default `run:` step); `GET /_fake/jobs/ID` of githome gives `github_job_id`, whose conclusion `GET /_fake/jobs/ID` of the fake GitHub gives. A run of `source-conformance` ends two jobs: queue three.
- podssh: `offer.fit(settings)` in `relay_layer.rs` in place of `move_bytes` alone, and a window of `window_under(cap)` for the far end of `relay serve` with `--max-session-mib`; then check in `layer_cap.rs` that no link ends with 1009.
- podnet and podvm: the tag only.
