# podcrates v0.80.0

The eightieth release of podcrates. It carries, for the CI of githome and
podrunner, the control API of `pod-control` (the control of `pod-broker`
moves onto it), the service units of `pod-unit`, and the bundle of a job
of `pod-broker` for Go, Java, Deno, pip, npm, gcloud, Hex and cargo on
macOS. For podvm, it carries the crate `pod-beneath`, the exec rule for
the program of a dynamic loader, the write table, the check of an argv,
one list of the signals of each relay of a payload, the saved state of any
machine line of `pod-guest`, and the static QEMU 11.0.0. It also carries
the fixes of `main` for podssh (the names of the chain of `pod-release`)
and for Windows (`PATHEXT`).

## Error texts

These lines change. A test that matches the old text gets the new one.

- `pod-release`, the chain of a signature (T-1016): `{name} (valid {from} to {to}) did not hold at the time of the signature ({time})`, `{name} was not issued by {issuer}: it names {issuer of the certificate}` (or `: it names no issuer`), `{name}'s signature by {issuer}: {why}`. A certificate with no subject is "the leaf certificate", "the root certificate" or "the intermediate certificate". Each line of the chain of the timestamp authority starts with `the chain of the timestamp authority: `. Each time is RFC 3339.
- `pod-enter`: a host path of a volume that goes above its base by `..` is `goes above its base by `..`` (`Class::Policy`), not "holds a symbolic link". A chain of six `#!` lines is `PATH: more than 5 levels of #! interpreters (ELOOP)`.
- `pod-confine`: the encode of a table with a `:` or a NUL byte in a path is `the path "P" of a rule holds a ':' or a NUL byte: the table of the paths cannot spell it`.
- `pod-broker`, the control: each answer is a line of `pod-control` protocol 1 (section "Breaking changes").

## Schemas

- `control.v1` (new): one line of the control API of a program (`pod-control`): a request (`schema`, `id`, `method`, `params`, `for`) or an answer (`schema`, `id`, `ok`, `result` or `error` with `code` and `detail`); the params and the results of each method of the catalogue under `$defs` (T-103).

## Pins

- The static QEMU of podvm's store: `pod-services`, the service `hermeticbuild-qemu`, gives QEMU 11.0.0 of `github.com/hermeticbuild/qemu-prebuilt`, the release `11.0.0.1`: the archives of `qemu-system-x86_64`, `qemu-system-aarch64`, `qemu-img` and the data (`share/qemu`), each with its SHA-256 and length, and each binary with its member, SHA-256 and length. It holds the QEMU commit `2865bf1c` (the fix of the use-after-free of `address_space_translate_for_iotlb`, PodUtils/podvm#618). The service `pkgforge-qemu` (QEMU 9.2.0 of `api.ghcr.pkgforge.dev`) is removed. QEMU 11.0 cannot save an NVMe device: the save of an NVMe disk needs QEMU 11.1.0 (T-1014, PodUtils/static-pkgs).

## Breaking changes

- `pod_enter::Names` has the field `write_table`: a caller that builds `Names` by its fields names it. podvm's `NAMES` literal (`crates/podvm-enter/src/lib.rs`) gets `write_table: "PODVM_WRITE_HOST"` (call C840).
- `pod_enter`: the entry with no chroot sets the variable `names.write_table` for a confined payload, and each entry removes a value of the caller.
- `pod_enter`: the child of each entry sets an empty signal mask before its `execve`. A payload no longer gets the mask of the thread that started it.
- `pod_broker::control`: `Broker::control_line`, `control::answer_failure` and `control::VERSION` are removed. `control::bind_unix` is `pod_broker::bind_unix`. `control::serve_control` takes `(Arc<Broker>, &Path, pod_control::Settings)` and gives a `ControlServer`; it is not `async`. The wire is `pod-control` protocol 1: a request `{"schema":1,"id":N,"method":"load","params":{...}}`, an answer `{"schema":1,"id":N,"ok":true,"result":{...}}` or `{"schema":1,"id":N,"ok":false,"error":{"code":CODE,"detail":WORDS}}`. `load` needs the master tier. A client of the old lines (`{"v":1,"op":...}`) gets no answer that it can read. No consumer reads this wire: podrunner and podvm call `Broker::load` and `Broker::drop_job` in their own process.
- `pod_broker::ca::env` gives ten variables: the six of `pod_tls::BUNDLE_VARS`, then the four of `ca::CLIENT_VARS`.
- `pod_confine::ExecTable` is a type alias of `PathTable`. `exec_table()`, `resolved()` and `decode()` give `PathTable`.
- `pod_hostprobe::sys::OpenHow` is `pod_beneath::OpenHow`: the same fields, and it also derives `Debug`, `PartialEq` and `Eq`.
- `pod_guest::qmp::Monitor`: each request ends with the member `"id":"pod-qmp-N"`. A stand-in that compares a request line as text gets the member. A `Monitor` is larger: an enum of a consumer that holds one can get the clippy finding `large_enum_variant`.
- `pod_guest::warm`: under KVM the `cpu` of a fingerprint adds `kernel` and the release of the kernel of the host. A state that `v0.79.0` saved under KVM is `Cold::Stale` once, and the guest boots cold.
- `pod_services`: the service `pkgforge-qemu` is removed; `hermeticbuild-qemu` has other facts (`release`, `fix-commit`, `NAME-member`, `NAME-bin-sha256`, `NAME-bin-bytes`, `data-sha256`, `data-bytes`).
- `pod_supervise`: the launcher gives SIGHUP, SIGQUIT, SIGUSR1, SIGUSR2, SIGALRM and SIGPWR to the payload; before, SIGQUIT, SIGUSR1, SIGUSR2, SIGALRM and SIGPWR ended the launcher, and the launcher gave SIGHUP to no process.
- The error texts of the section "Error texts".

No enum of a public API that a consumer matches got a new variant. The new enums are `pod_broker::control::Ending`, `pod_core::process::RunFault`, `pod_guest::warm::line::Transport` and the enums of `pod-control`, `pod-unit` and `pod-beneath`.
