# podcrates v0.22.0

The twenty-second release of podcrates. For podssh: `pod-e2e`, the
end-to-end channel (Noise XX) cut from podssh's `e2e/`, and the TCP relay
of ZeroTier in `pod-services`. For podnet: `pod-wg`, the WireGuard
initiator of WARP with no I/O. For podvm and podrunner: a payload ends with
a killed launcher (`Plan.die_with_parent`), and `wait` and `stop` of
`pod-supervise` tell a gone launcher from the bound.

## Crates

## How a consumer takes it

Each podcrates dependency names the tag `v0.22.0`.

- podssh applies `adoption/podssh/pod-e2e.patch` (its trial passes at
  podssh `74760e8`, adoption 43008801789): `crates/podssh-relay/src/e2e/`
  becomes the glue of the process (`node`, `say`, the net's documents and
  the holds), and podssh adds its own hints where it prints `NotChannel`
  and `NO_EXIT`. Its ZeroTier road reads
  `pod_services::get("zerotier-tcp-relay")`.
- podnet applies `adoption/podnet/pod-wg.patch` (its trial at podnet
  `54d7ed4`): `src/warp/wg.rs` holds `pod_wg::Tunnel`, `connect_on` sends
  the mac2 of a cookie reply in its next try, and `Peer`, `Sock` and the
  tasks stay in podnet.
- podvm sets `Plan.die_with_parent = true` in the entry that
  `pod-supervise`'s launcher calls, so a payload ends with a killed
  launcher.

## Breaking

- `pod_enter::Plan` has the field `die_with_parent`: a struct literal
  names it.
- `pod_supervise::wait` gives `Dead` (no exit code) where it gave the old
  record `Running` with no code after a launcher was killed; `stop` gives
  `false` there, not `true`.

## Known defect

- `pod-session`: `a_session_moves_while_both_ends_send_at_full_speed` lost
  its link one time in a run of the workspace's tests (T-204, open).
- `pod-netstack`: the reactor of `NetStack` gives one ACK for the segments
  that one poll takes (T-197, open).
- `pod-gsr` on Windows ARM64: a test process ends with
  `STATUS_ACCESS_VIOLATION` (T-131, open).

## Publish
