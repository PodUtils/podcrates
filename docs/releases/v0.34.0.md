# podcrates v0.34.0

The thirty-fourth release of podcrates. For podnet: the TCP carrier of
`pod-link` with TLS 1.3 and the pin of an Ed25519 key (T-304, T-396,
PodUtils/podnet#513, for its T-268), the settings of its stack of `ip.rs`
in `pod-warp` (T-335), and the flows of a closed WARP session that fail
at once (T-394). For each consumer: the downloads of CI from a cache
(T-355).

## Crates

| Crate | Change | Consumers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `pod-tls` | new module `peer`: a client configuration that takes one server key and a server configuration that requires a client key of a shared set (`Keys`), each named as `pod_identity::KeyName` names a key; each side presents the self-signed Ed25519 certificate of its `pod-identity` key; TLS 1.3, Ed25519, no resumption; a refused key is `Refusal::Unpinned` with the key seen (`refusal(&err)`); calls C175, C176 (T-304) | podnet |
| `pod-link` | feature `tcp`: `tcp::Tcp` dials `KEY@HOST:PORT` through the caller's `Arc<dyn pod_core::Dial>` and listens on `IP:PORT` with the shared set of client keys; at most 64 handshakes at once; a byte of acceptance after the handshake, so a refused dialer fails at its dial; the ALPN `podlink/1`; `Listener::accept_peer` gives the key of the peer (the unix and the memory carriers give none); a refused key is `Refused` (exit 77); calls C177 to C179 (T-396) | podnet |
| `pod-netstack` | `StackConfig::congestion_control`, `max_burst` and `link_stats` (T-335, C170); `NetStack::reset_all(reason)` and `resetter()` end each open stream and UDP socket with `ConnectionReset` and the reason; the stop of the reactor (a shutdown, a drop, a panic) ends each open socket with its cause (`stop_reason`, `stopped`); a write that waited for room when its connection ended now wakes (T-394, C180) | podnet, podvm |
| `pod-warp` | `NetSettings` (`Settings::net`): the MTU, the TCP buffer of 512 KiB, the congestion controller, the TCP burst, the counters of the link, the check of the data path, the budget of a relay (T-335, C170 to C174); the first cause of a close resets the stack, a stop of the reactor closes the session, and `WarpNet::close(why)` closes it by the caller (T-394, C181) | podnet |

## How a consumer takes it

Each podcrates dependency names the tag `v0.34.0`.

- podnet: `pod-link` with `features = ["tcp"]` for its T-268:
  `Tcp::new(provider, identity, way).with_clients(Keys::new(names))`,
  `dial("FINGERPRINT@HOST:PORT")`, `listen("IP:PORT")` and
  `accept_peer()`; `pod_link::tcp::refusal(&e)` names the key of a
  refusal. `pod-warp`: the settings of its `ip.rs` (`NetSettings`) and
  `WarpNet::close(why)` for its `--session-max-age`; its adoption trial
  stays T-339.
- podssh, podvm and podrunner: move the tag.

## Breaking

- `pod-netstack`: a read after the stop of the stack returns
  `ConnectionReset` with the cause, not `Ok(0)`.
- `pod-tls` and `pod-link` depend on `pod-identity`.

## Known defect

- `pod-netstack`: the reactor gives one ACK for the segments of one poll
  (T-197, open).
- `pod-session`: a random failure of one test under load (T-204, open).
- `pod-gsr` on Windows ARM64: `STATUS_ACCESS_VIOLATION` (T-131, open).

## Publish

`publish.yml` runs on the tag: the dry run of `cargo publish --workspace`.
The switch `crates-io` of `Cargo.toml` is false (the owner, 2026-11-09).
`pod-link`, `pod-turn`, `pod-devtunnel`, `pod-warp` and `pod-netlab` are
not reserved on crates.io yet: the owner reserves them before a publish.
