# podcrates v0.27.0

The twenty-seventh release of podcrates. For podnet: the close of a
listener and of a stream, and no RST for a burst of SYNs, in
`pod-netstack` (PodUtils/podnet#457); podnet's relay client in
`pod-relay` (T-026, partial). For podssh: the trial of `pod-relay`. For
podvm: a rule of `Plan.confine` covers the paths that the child mounts
(PodUtils/podvm#267), `pod-services` lists `mcr.microsoft.com`
(PodUtils/podvm#269), and a closed socket of `pod-confine` closes the
sockets that Landlock does not check.

## How a consumer takes it

Each podcrates dependency names the tag `v0.27.0`.

- podnet: its PodUtils/podnet@T-249 drops `StackConfig::accept_pool` and
  its cap of 256 listeners, closes each listener when its last flow ends,
  and drops each stream with no wait for `StackEvent::Closed`; then its
  COORD issue with the commit starts T-170 step 2 here. `pod-relay` has
  podnet's relay client; its trial for podnet comes after T-234.
- podssh applies `adoption/podssh/pod-relay.patch` (its trial at podssh
  `75a5031`, passed in `adoption.yml`): podssh-relay's modules of the
  relay client become glue over `pod-relay`. The patch changes
  `pinggy::dashboard()`; keep your `pinggy::skips()` of `ff2f7bac`.
- podvm: no change of its code. A rule on a path that a mount of the
  child hides is now refused at the entry; run the `/tmp` clause of
  experiment 414. Its host test can drop `mcr.microsoft.com` from its
  list of reported hosts.
- podrunner moves the tag.

## Breaking

- `pod_netstack::StackConfig`: `accept_pool` is gone; `linger` is new.
  `max_connections` counts the half-open connections. A RST of the peer
  reads as `ConnectionReset`. The drop of a listener frees its port.
  `NetStats::dropped` counts the SYNs that waited at the limit.
- `pod_relay::reverse::Settings` has `half_close` and `drain`, and
  `OperatorLimits` has `eof_idle` (a struct literal with no `..` names
  them). `failover::MODE_ENV` is gone.
- `pod_confine::Call` has `OpenPath`. A spec with a TCP list or a closed
  UDP refuses the socket families that Landlock does not check.

## Known defect

- `pod-confine`: on a target with `socketcall(2)` (i686, s390x,
  powerpc64) the rules of a TCP list do not see a socket made through it.
- `pod-netstack`: the reactor gives one ACK for the segments of one poll
  (T-197, open).
- `pod-session`: a random failure of one test under load (T-204, open;
  1 in 200 runs under four busy cores).
- `pod-gsr` on Windows ARM64: `STATUS_ACCESS_VIOLATION` (T-131, open).
- No watch reads the banner of an SSH forward relay yet (T-225, open).
