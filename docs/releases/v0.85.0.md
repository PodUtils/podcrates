# podcrates v0.85.0

The eighty-fifth release of podcrates. It carries each change of `main`
after `v0.84.0`. It has no new crate. For podrunner and podvm, it carries
the PATCH of the config of an incus instance on the condition of its ETag
(`pod-incus`, PodUtils/podvm#664 for PodUtils/podrunner#354), and the port
of podrunner `fcff5bc` into `pod-testkit`. For podnet, it carries the rule
that the text of a failure is its step and its cause says the rest
(`pod-core`, `pod-cloudflare`, `pod-quic`, `pod-warp`), and the random temp
names of seven crates (PodUtils/podnet#711, partial). For podssh, it carries
`Bearer::to_bytes` of `pod-relay` and one proxy node less in
`pod-services`.

## Breaking changes

A consumer reads this section first. Each item gives the fix.

- `pod_core::Failure::io` takes a step: `Failure::io(stage, step, e)` (T-1351).
  The text of the failure is the step, and the error of I/O is its cause. A
  call `Failure::io(stage, e)` does not build: give the step that the error
  ended, for example `Failure::io(Stage::Connect, "the connection", e)`. The
  class is the class of the kind of `e`, as before.
- The `Display` of a failure of `Failure::io` is the step alone, not the
  words of the error of I/O (T-1351). The crates of this repository that make
  such failures give a step now: `pod-broker`, `pod-cloudflare`,
  `pod-extract`, `pod-gsr`, `pod-natprobe`, `pod-proxy`, `pod-quic`,
  `pod-unit`, `pod-warp` and `pod-ws` (section "Error texts").
  `Failure::chain()` (or `{:#}` of anyhow) gives the step and the cause. A
  line for a user takes `chain()`. A line that shows `to_string()` or
  `text()` of such a failure loses the words of the error.
- The failures of `pod-cloudflare`, `pod-quic` and `pod-warp` that held the
  words of their typed cause in their text now hold the step alone, and the
  cause says the rest (T-1351):
  - `From<ProvisionError> for Failure`: the text is
    `quicktunnel::PROVISIONING` ("provisioning quick tunnel"), and the
    `ProvisionError` is the source.
  - `no_leg_worked` ("no edge leg worked: ..."): the text of the last leg
    stops before its typed cause. The source is that typed cause (a
    `Refused` of the registration, an `io::Error`), not the `Failure` of the
    last leg. `fail::find::<Refused>` finds the refusal as before. A reader
    that downcasts the source to `Failure` gets `None` there.
  - The doors of `pod-warp` (`each_door_failed`), the join of the direct
    path and the relay, and `host::sent` of `pod-cloudflare`: the source is
    the typed root cause, not a `Failure`.
- `pod_cloudflare::fail::tls_text(&io::Error)` gives `Option<&'static str>`:
  the words that a TLS failure adds, or `None` where the error says it all.
  It gave a `String` with the words of the error. Call `fail::tls_step(step,
  &e)` for the text of a failure whose cause is `e` (T-1351). No consumer
  calls it (podnet has its own `hop::tls_text`).
- `pod_todo::write::next_id(root)` is `next_id(root, from)`: the next free
  id at or above `T-{from}` (T-1385). Give `1` for the old result. No
  consumer calls it; `cargo todo next` with no argument gives the same id.
- `pod_incus::ErrorKind` has the new variant `EtagMismatch { error }`
  (T-1379). The enum is `non_exhaustive`: each `match` has a `_` arm and
  builds.
- The temp paths of seven crates have a random part (T-1343). A reader that
  finds a path by its old name (the pid, the clock or a counter) does not
  find it. The new names are in the section "Crates". The sweep of podvm
  finds the temp table of `pod-supervise` by the extension `.partial`, which
  stays.
- The error texts of the sections below.

## Error texts

These lines change. A test that matches the old text gets the new one. In
each line, `ERROR` is the error of I/O: it is in `Failure::chain()`, not in
the `Display`.

- `pod-cloudflare`: the failure of a provision is "provisioning quick tunnel"; its chain is "provisioning quick tunnel: ERROR". The failure of each leg: "no edge leg worked: LEG: CHAIN; LEG: STEPS", and the cause of the last leg is the source. The TLS of a leg of HTTP/2: "TLS with the edge at ADDR: the connection was reset during the TLS handshake", "... was closed during the TLS handshake", "... the peer sent a TLS alert" (it was "TLS alert from the peer: ALERT"), or "TLS with the edge at ADDR" alone; the error is the cause. A request through `host::sent`: "REQUEST: TLS: WORDS". The UDP socket of QUIC: "could not open a local UDP socket (QUIC needs UDP; where it is blocked, use PROTOCOL http2)"; it was "could not open a local UDP socket: no UDP socket for QUIC: ERROR. QUIC needs UDP; where it is blocked, use PROTOCOL http2". The warning of a retry of the provision: "provisioning failed: CHAIN; the try N is in S s".
- `pod-quic`: "no UDP socket for QUIC" and "no QUIC endpoint"; they were "no UDP socket for QUIC: ERROR" and "no QUIC endpoint: ERROR".
- `pod-warp`: "TLS handshake with the MASQUE front door DOOR"; it was "TLS handshake with the MASQUE front door DOOR: ERROR". The doors: "DOOR-A CHAIN; the other address of the front door failed too: DOOR-B STEPS"; it was "DOOR-A TEXT; the other address of the front door failed too (DOOR-B CHAIN)". A pin mismatch: "the MASQUE key is not the pinned key, and PROVIDER keeps no device record to read again" and "the MASQUE key is not the pinned key, and the device record read again has no MASQUE front door"; the mismatch is the source. "the trace request to ADDRESS" and "binding UDP port PORT in the WARP stack".
- `pod-broker`: "reading PATH" (the policy and the deny list), "the answer to the job", "the exchange", "the exchange with the upstream", "the exchange with the job", "reading the job", "reading the upstream", "the TLS handshake of the job", "the tunnel".
- `pod-unit`: "making DIR", "writing PATH", "reading PATH", "removing PATH".
- `pod-gsr`: "reading a message of the relay", "writing to the relay", "the SRP handshake", "starting the runtime of the blocking stream".
- `pod-ws`: "writing to the local stream", "reading the local stream" (the bridge).
- `pod-proxy`: "system resolver" (a failure of the system resolver).
- `pod-natprobe`: "binding UDP ADDR", "the local address of the UDP socket".
- `pod-extract`: "the extraction" (a failure of I/O of an extraction).
- `pod-incus`: a 412 to a write with `If-Match`: "CALL: the instance changed after the read of its ETag (412): ERROR OF INCUS".
- `pod-enter`: a mount directory that cannot be made names the base directory, not the new name. The refusal "the staging directory already exists" is gone: a name that is there takes a new name.
