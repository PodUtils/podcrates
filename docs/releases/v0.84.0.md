# podcrates v0.84.0

The eighty-fourth release of podcrates. It carries each change of `main`
after `v0.83.0`. It has three new crates: `pod-tpm`, `pod-gzip` and
`pod-h2`. For podrunner, it carries the fields `ref` and `repo_id` of
`RealJob` and the warm units of `run-report.v1` and `status.v1`. For
podssh, it carries `pod-tpm`, the order of the pseudo-fields of the HTTP/2
request of `pod-ws` (the fork `pod-h2`), the bare form and the DNS over HTTPS
form of the DNS carrier of `pod-link`, the version 2 of the transfer codec of
`pod-term`, the bearer grant of `pod-relay` and `pod-e2e`, `Mint::sign_with`,
and the class `Data`. For podvm, it carries `flatten` of `pod-extract`,
`pod-gzip`, and the write bound and the `cut` field of `pod-incus`. For
podnet, it carries the replies of the WARP proxy that has no session, the
cause by issuer of the door of `pod-warp`, the one cause of each failure of
`pod-cloudflare`, and `new_temp_dir` and `new_temp_file` of `pod-core`.

## Breaking changes

A consumer reads this section first. Each item gives the fix.

- `pod_core::Class` has the new variant `Data` (name `data`, code 65,
  `EX_DATAERR`) at the end of `Class::ALL`, which has 16 items (T-1238,
  PodUtils/podssh#352). A `match` on `Class` with no `_` arm does not build.
  podssh's `code_of` (`devtunnel.rs`) adds `Class::Data`. podnet's
  `code_of_class` adds `DATA_ERROR`. A `match` with a `_` arm builds.
  `pod-manual` has the meaning of `data` in `failure_classes`. The schema
  `log.v1` has `data` in `class`.
- The `Display` of a `pod_core::Failure` that `pod-cloudflare` makes gives its
  own step alone, not the step and its causes (T-1135, PodUtils/podnet#707).
  `Failure::chain()` (or `{:#}` of anyhow) gives the step and the causes, each
  cause once. A line for a user takes `chain()`. podnet's `exit::text` and the
  rows of its `--check` read the chain. The `Display` of
  `pod_cloudflare::fail::Refused` no longer starts with "the edge refused the
  registration: "; `From<Refused> for Failure` has that text as its step.
- The workspace dependency `h2` is the fork `pod-h2` (T-1095, call C934).
  Each crate of the workspace that depends on h2 (`pod-ws`, `pod-link`,
  `pod-dns`, `pod-relay`, `pod-egress`, `pod-cloudflare`, `pod-warp`,
  `pod-wormhole`, `pod-piping`, `pod-services-watch` and `pod-testkit`) now
  uses the types of `pod-h2`. A public item that names an h2 type, as
  `pod_warp::session::h2_builder` and `pod_cloudflare::edge_h2`, takes the
  fork's type. A consumer that
  passes an h2 type of crates.io to one of them, for example a stream to
  `pod_ws::h2_road::duplex`, takes the type from `pod_ws::h2`, or names the
  fork in its manifest: `h2 = { package = "pod-h2", git = "https://github.com/PodUtils/podcrates", tag = "v0.84.0" }`.
  A consumer with its own fork of h2 and a `[patch.crates-io]` line removes
  both (podssh: `vendor/h2`). The test `h2_fallback.rs` of podssh calls
  `h2::decode_header_block` of the fork.
- `pod_job::source::RealJob` has the new fields `git_ref: Option<String>` (the
  wire name `ref`) and `repo_id: Option<u64>` (T-1362,
  PodUtils/podrunner#352). `pod_job::UnitFields` has the new fields `start`,
  `warm_s` and `boot_s`. `pod_job::KindStatus` has the new field `warm`.
  `pod_job::Status` has the new field `warm_units` (default 0) (T-1370,
  PodUtils/podrunner#353). A struct literal that names each field adds them:
  `git_ref: None, repo_id: None` for `RealJob`; `start: None, warm_s: None,
  boot_s: None` for `UnitFields`; `warm: None` for `KindStatus`;
  `warm_units: 0` for `Status`. A field that is `None` is not on the wire.
- The wire of the relay is version 2 (T-1367, T-1368, PodUtils/podssh#355).
  Version 2 adds the bearer of a grant (`docs/wire.md`, section 6, "Bearers").
  No byte of a message of version 1 changes. The files of
  `crates/pod-relay/tests/vectors/` say `"wire": 2`, and the page has a new
  digest. An implementation that holds the vectors takes the new files.
- The transfer codec of `pod-term` is version 2 (T-1198, PodUtils/podssh#353).
  The trigger is `#PODSSH-TX2:`, and the `HELO` has the version 2. An end of
  version 1 and an end of version 2 do not start a session: the near end
  gives `Event::Unsupported { version }`, and a far end of version 2 that
  reads a `HELO` of version 1 stops with `stop::UNSUPPORTED`, not
  `stop::PROTOCOL`. Both ends of a link take this tag. podssh has not wired
  the codec yet (PodUtils/podssh@T-831).
- `pod_cloudflare::standin::edge::listen` is removed. Call
  `edge::listen_at(addr)`, which gives an `io::Result`. `Standin::start_at(edge)`
  starts the stand-ins with the edge on the address (T-1360). The feature
  `standin` is for tests.
- `pod_incus::Settings` has the new public field `write_timeout` (30 s) (T-1298).
  `pod_incus::ErrorKind::Incus` has the new field `cut: Option<u64>` (T-1307).
  A struct literal of `Settings` adds the first. A `match` of `Incus` with all
  its fields and no `..` adds the second. podvm uses `Settings::new` and does
  not match `Incus` by all its fields.
- `pod_extract::safety::Refusal` has three new variants: `TooLong(usize)`,
  `Stream(String)` and `Link(String)` (T-1193, T-1194, T-1195). The enum is
  not `non_exhaustive`: an exhaustive `match` adds three arms.
  `pod_extract::apply::LayerStats` has the new field `replaced`
  (the struct has `Default`).
- `pod_harness::zip::crc32` is removed (T-1197). Call `crc32fast::hash`.
- The error texts of the sections below.

## Error texts

These lines change. A test that matches the old text gets the new one.

- `pod-cloudflare`: the `Display` of a failure is its step alone (`provisioning quick tunnel`, `the edge refused the registration`); `chain()` gives the step and each cause once.
- `pod-warp`: the line of a proxy client with no session is `no WARP session: CAUSE` (HTTP status 503). The line of a door that shows another key has one sentence for each issuer class: `WARP's side shows a certificate of Let's Encrypt at times in place of its key; a later try passes then`, and `A middlebox on the way terminates TLS; the key is pinned, so no root helps: go around it`. The cause comes once.
- `pod-term`: a `HELO` of another version stops the far end with the reason `HELO of version 1; this end takes version 2`, with the code `stop::UNSUPPORTED`. It gave `stop::PROTOCOL`.
- `pod-incus`: a body over the bound that closes before its end is `TooLarge` with the words of the start of the envelope, not "the connection closed in the middle of the response body".

## How a consumer takes it
