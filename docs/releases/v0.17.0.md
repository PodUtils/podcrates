# podcrates v0.17.0

The seventeenth release of podcrates: `pod-job` (the records of a CI
runner's job and their JSON Schemas) for podrunner; the resource limits and
`no_new_privs` of a payload in `pod-enter` for podvm; the hosts of GitHub
Actions and the pin of the official runner in `pod-services`, and mutual
TLS with pinned keys in `pod-tls`, for podrunner.

## Crates

| Crate | Change | Consumers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `pod-job` | new: the job offer, the unit capability, the run report and the public report, their ids, the refusal codes and the checks that serde does not do, from podrunner's `crates/podrunner-schema` at `9ab7fd3`; the four schemas `schemas/job-offer.v1.schema.json`, `unit-capability.v1`, `run-report.v1` and `run-report-public.v1`, generated from the types by a test (schemars only as a dev-dependency or the feature `schema`). Fixed against the draft: the schemas hold `schema` 1, the arch, an image by digest, and the rules of the limits and the reports; the job id pattern has no look-around; `PublicReport::check`. | `pod-enter` | `Plan::limits` (`Limit`, `Resource`) and `Plan::no_new_privs`: the child of each entry sets them after the `chdir` and before the exec; a refused call names itself (`setrlimit(RLIMIT_NOFILE): EPERM`) (T-176, PodUtils/podvm#245) | podvm (`--ulimit`, `--security-opt no-new-privileges`) |
| `pod-hostprobe` | `sys::set_rlimit`, `sys::set_no_new_privs`, `sys::rlimit` (the numbers of the kernel of each architecture) (T-176) | `pod-enter` |

| `pod-services` | `github-actions` (the `actions_inbound` domains of GitHub's meta API, watched each day; the multi-tenant wildcards flagged) and `github-actions-runner` (the pin of `actions/runner`, a watch of 30 days) (T-164) | podrunner (PodUtils/podrunner#24, #30) |

## How a consumer takes it

Each podcrates dependency names the tag `v0.17.0`. podrunner moves
`podrunner_schema::X` to `pod_job::X` and removes its copies of the types
and the schemas (`crates/pod-job/README.md`, "Adoption map"). podvm sets
`limits` and `no_new_privs` of each `Plan` (empty and false keep the old
behaviour).

## Breaking

- `pod_enter::Plan` has the fields `limits` and `no_new_privs`: a struct
  literal names them.

## Known defect

- `pod-gsr` on Windows ARM64: a test process ends with
  `STATUS_ACCESS_VIOLATION` (T-131, open).
- Three tests of `pod-enter` fail under qemu-user only (T-149, open).

## Publish

`publish.yml` runs on the tag: the dry run of `cargo publish --workspace`.
The switch `crates-io` of `Cargo.toml` is false (the owner, 2026-11-09).
`pod-job` waits for its name on crates.io (GitHub #1, "To register").
