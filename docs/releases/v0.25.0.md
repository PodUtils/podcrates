# podcrates v0.25.0

The twenty-fifth release of podcrates. For podnet: the calls of
`pod-netstack` that its netns router needs (PodUtils/podnet#454). For
podvm and podrunner: `pod-confine` closes the Landlock scopes, can close
UDP, refuses MPTCP under a TCP list and io_uring, and drops the
capabilities of a root payload that the spec does not keep. For podssh:
`pod-relay`, imported from podssh and adapted; podssh's take comes with
the trial of its glue.

## Crates

| Crate | Change | Consumers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `pod-netstack` | `StackConfig::any_ip` (the stack answers for each destination), `StackConfig::checksum` and `LinkDevice::with_checksum` (`Checksum::Tx` for a veth wire), `NetStack::listen_any(port)` (a TCP listener on any address; the `local_addr()` of an accepted stream is the address that the program dialed), `NetStack::bind_udp_any(port)` with `UdpSocket::send_to_from(buf, remote, source)` and `recv_from_to(buf)` (the destination of each datagram). Fixed: a UDP send to an address of the other family panicked smoltcp and stopped the stack; it is `InvalidInput` now (T-222) | podnet |
| `pod-confine` | `Spec::scope` (on: the Landlock scopes of ABI 6, signals and abstract Unix sockets), `Spec::udp` (`close_udp()`: a UDP socket gets `EACCES` and the io_uring calls get `EPERM`; refused by name on a target with `socketcall`), no MPTCP under a TCP list (`ENOPROTOOPT`), and the default deny list denies io_uring (calls C61 to C63; T-209). `Spec::keep_caps`: `apply` drops each capability that the mask does not name; `Spec::new()` keeps docker's default set (`DOCKER_CAPS`, the owner's ruling "Root caps"), `no_caps()` keeps none, `keep_cap(n)` adds one. A root payload with `CAP_SYS_ADMIN` or `CAP_PERFMON` read the environment of its unconfined ancestors past the Landlock domain; the default set holds neither (T-221). `Deny` holds a list of conditions (`when_flags`, `when_equal`) | podvm, podrunner |
| `pod-hostprobe` | `capget`, `capset`, `capbset_drop`, `ambient_clear`, the capability numbers; the socket, scope and io_uring constants (T-209, T-221) | each |
| `pod-relay` | new: the relay client, the pool, the rank, the roads and the reverse road of podssh, imported at podssh `75a5031` and adapted: one `Context` of the caller in place of podssh's `ProxyChoice` and process globals, `Settings`, `Names`, `NodeState` (call C60). 356 tests, 28 plants of podssh. podssh's take comes with its trial (T-025, partial) | podssh (later podnet, podvm) |
| `pod-enter` | a test only: dash takes `>&N` for one digit (T-216) | podvm |

## How a consumer takes it

Each podcrates dependency names the tag `v0.25.0`.

- podnet starts its T-249 (the router onto `pod-netstack`) with the calls
  above; its COORD issue with the commit starts T-170 step 2 here.
- podvm sets `Plan.confine` from its profiles (PodUtils/podvm@T-1658);
  `Spec::new()` keeps docker's capabilities, so a root payload that
  installs packages works. Say on #69 if a profile needs a key for
  `scope`, `udp` or the kept capabilities.
- podrunner: its T-042 builds its `Spec` with `Spec::new()` and the
  builders; its test 27 can run as root too.
- podssh: no change now; the patch of `pod-relay` comes with its trial.

## Breaking

- `pod_confine::Spec` has the fields `scope`, `udp` and `keep_caps`; a
  struct literal names them. A confined root payload keeps docker's
  capabilities and no other one.
- `pod_confine::Deny`: `flags` is `when: Vec<Arg>`; a rule that does not
  hold passes the call to the next rule of the same number.
- `pod_netstack::StackConfig` has the fields `any_ip` and `checksum`. A
  socket on any address and one on one address of the same port are
  `AddrInUse`; a UDP send to the other family is `InvalidInput`.

## Known defect

- `pod-confine`: SCTP, SMC, Unix sockets with a path and the UDP rights of
  a newer Landlock are not covered under a TCP list or a closed UDP
  (T-213, open).
- `pod-netstack`: the reactor gives one ACK for the segments of one poll
  (T-197, open); a SYN sent again can reach a newer listen socket of the
  pool of a listener (T-223, open).
- `pod-session`: a random failure of one test under load (T-204, open).
- `pod-gsr` on Windows ARM64: `STATUS_ACCESS_VIOLATION` (T-131, open).
