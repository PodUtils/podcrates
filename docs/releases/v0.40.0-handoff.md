## podnet

- PodUtils/podnet#515 and #521: the carriers of `pod-link`:
  - `poll` (the long poll, SSE, a chunked stream and the simplex poll over HTTP/1.1; `Poll::max_bytes` caps the bytes down that a listener keeps, 64 MiB);
  - `quic` (one bidirectional stream of a QUIC connection of `pod-quic`);
  - `udp` (KCP over UDP or your datagrams, TLS above);
  - `dns` (the ARQ in base32 labels of queries and in TXT answers, `KEY@ZONE`, an authoritative listener);
  - `icmp` (the ARQ in ICMP echo, `KEY@HOST`, a raw socket with `CAP_NET_RAW` on unix).
  Each carrier has its own feature. Calls C266 to C280.

Set `window: MAX_WINDOW` with `?kcp-nodelay=1`.
- #139 (T-526): set `[profile.dev.package.kcp] overflow-checks = false` in your root `Cargo.toml` with this take. Answer on #139.
