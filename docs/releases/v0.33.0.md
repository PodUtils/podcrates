# podcrates v0.33.0

The thirty-third release of podcrates. For podssh: `pod-devtunnel`, the
client side of Microsoft Dev Tunnels (T-378, PodUtils/podssh#216), and
`pod-turn` (T-377, GitHub #100). For podnet: `pod-turn` (PodUtils/podnet#485),
the first part of `pod-warp` (T-028, its WARP chain, PodUtils/podnet#449),
and its asks A11 and A12 (T-390, T-391, PodUtils/podnet#512). For podvm:
the offline part of `pod-netlab` (T-207, PodUtils/podvm#260).

## How a consumer takes it

Each podcrates dependency names the tag `v0.33.0`.

- podssh: `pod-devtunnel` for its T-755 (`Locator::parse`, `UserToken`,
  `Relay::connect`, `Relay::open`, `step_of` for its doctor; the stand-in
  of the feature `standin` for its test); `pod_proxy::stun` keeps each
  name, so its TURN road and its doctor need only the tag.
- podnet: `pod-turn` for its `turn://` (`Settings::udp` or
  `Settings::tls` over its dialer and TLS context); `pod-devtunnel` for its
  `devtunnel://`; `pod-warp` for its WARP chain (the first part; its
  adoption trial is T-339); `GaveUp::cause` for its `ResumeExpired`;
  `Allowlist::parse_with` and the display form for its allow list.
- podvm: move the tag; `pod-netlab` for its T-1662 is the offline part.
- podrunner: move the tag (its session waits for podvm).

## Breaking

- `pod_session::End::GaveUp` holds a `GaveUp`, not a `String`: read
  `.reason`.
- `pod-proxy` no longer depends on `hmac` and `sha1` (the codec moved).

## Known defect

- `pod-netstack`: the reactor gives one ACK for the segments of one poll
  (T-197, open).
- `pod-session`: a random failure of one test under load (T-204, open).
- `pod-gsr` on Windows ARM64: `STATUS_ACCESS_VIOLATION` (T-131, open).

## Publish

`publish.yml` runs on the tag: the dry run of `cargo publish --workspace`.
The switch `crates-io` of `Cargo.toml` is false (the owner, 2026-11-09).
`pod-link`, `pod-turn`, `pod-devtunnel`, `pod-warp` and `pod-netlab` are
not reserved on crates.io yet: the owner reserves them before a publish.
