# podcrates v0.72.0

The seventy-second release of podcrates. It carries the move of
podrunner's status and source wire into `pod-job` (githome's G-31), one
reader of an HTTP/1.1 head for each crate, the byte budget of the layer
under a small cap of a relay (a gate of podssh's beta), the `PT_INTERP`
of a payload past its head (Fedora 42 on podvm's userland rung), and a
refused address that works on macOS.

## Crates

| Crate | Change | Consumers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `pod-job` | The types of podrunner's `status.v1` (`Status`, `SourceStatus`, `KindStatus`, `PinStatus`) and of its source wire (`pod_job::source`: `Request`, `Reply`, `Event`, `Offer`, `RealJob`, `HostFacts`, `KindFacts`, `SourceCode`, `SourceError`, `Minted`, `MintedFile`, `Hidden`; `parse_request`, `parse_reply`, `request_line`, `reply_line`), from podrunner at `ca8c308`, with `schemas/status.v1`, `source-request.v1` and `source-reply.v1` and their examples. A minted credential is `Minted`, whose content is `Hidden` (its `Debug` prints its length, the value is zeroized at the drop). `Reply::Done` is `Done {}`, and `status.v1` refuses an unknown field (T-922, GitHub #223 G-31, PodUtils/podrunner#294) | podrunner, githome |
| `schemas/` | Each schema comes from its types (`broker-policy.v1` and `log.v1` too); `schemas/CHANGES.md` has one row for each change of a schema, and `scripts/check-schemas.py` fails on a change with no row, and on a change that the ruling "Schema ids" gives a new id that keeps the old one (T-922) | githome, each project |
| `pod-core` | `http1`: one parser and writer of an HTTP/1.1 head with its bounds (`Bounds`, `parse_request`, `parse_head`, `parse_status_line`, `request_head`, `response_head`), in place of the copies of `pod-proxy`, `pod-ws`, `pod-piping`, `pod-relay`, `pod-egress`, `pod-incus` and `pod-lab`; a request line with a control character is refused, and `Authorization` keeps its value (T-882, PodUtils/podrunner#289) | each project |
| `pod-relay` | The budget of the layer stays under each cap of a relay (the larger of the cap less the margin and three quarters of the cap); `forward_layer::Offer` carries `max_session_bytes` of `/relays.json`, kept in the cache, and `Offer::move_bytes()` gives the move threshold for `pod-session` (T-909, PodUtils/podssh#314 ask 2) | podssh, podnet |
| `pod-session` | A link is lost when a PING waits 30 s for its answer and neither the bytes received nor the bytes acknowledged move: the layer resumes on a new link (T-939, call C784, PodUtils/podssh#283 ask 4). The wire does not change | podssh, podnet |
| `pod-iroh` | `home_relay::say_changes(endpoint, say)`: a line when the relay connection of the client ends, is dialled again, fails, and comes back (T-939) | podssh |
| `pod-enter` | `abi::interp_of_open`, `ladder::Payload::interp` and `memfd::eligible_payload` read a `PT_INTERP` path that lies past the head from the open file, so Fedora 42's programs (`PT_INTERP` at `0xf2000`) are dynamic, not "unknown" (T-944, PodUtils/podvm#589) | podvm |
| `pod-testkit` | `refused::refused()` gives a port with no socket on macOS, where the kernel drops a SYN to a bound socket that does not listen (T-936) | podnet, each project |
| `pod-cloudflare` | It speaks cloudflared `2026.11.1` (T-930); the doc of `Config::ha_connections` says why a quick tunnel takes 2 (T-931); three tests run on Windows (T-933) | podnet |
| `pod-services` | The Syncthing relay `DVWOU27` at `61.223.4.2` (T-930) | podnet, podssh |

## Tests and checks

- New plants: `core-http1-control-in-request-line`, `broker-schema-not-written`, `broker-schema-open-with-rules`, `schemas-change-with-no-row`, `schemas-removed-field-keeps-the-id`, `schemas-added-field-with-no-row`, `source-done-takes-any-field`, `status-schema-not-written`, `relay-layer-budget-ignores-the-cap`, `enter-interp-from-the-head-alone`, `session-t939-ping-unanswered`, `iroh-t939-relay-back-unsaid`. Two plants that a run could miss run their test five times.
- The fuzz target `http_request`. `ci.yml` builds the static probe of `aarch64-apple-darwin` on the Intel runner of macOS (T-943). The riscv64 job of `nightly.yml` has 150 minutes (T-938).

## How a consumer takes it

Each podcrates dependency names the tag `v0.72.0`; `issue-sync.yml@v0.72.0` takes `podcrates_ref: v0.72.0`.

- podrunner: take the status and source wire from `pod_job` and `pod_job::source` (`crates/pod-job/README.md`, "Adoption map"): convert between `Minted` and your `Credential`; `Done {}`; your `status.v1` output passes the strict schema. Remove your `schemas/status.v1.json`, its test and the three wire tests that moved; your `CHANGES.md` lines are in `schemas/CHANGES.md`. Take `pod_core::http1` in place of `podrunner-hook/src/http.rs`.
- podssh: set `Settings { move_bytes: offer.move_bytes(), .. }` from the offer (read or cached) in `relay_layer.rs`; an `Offer` literal adds `..Offer::default()`.
- podssh: call `pod_iroh::home_relay::say_changes` on the client's endpoint with your verbose line; a link can now end with "the far end answered no PING and acknowledged nothing for 30 s"; run your loop of `m6_exit` again (T-945 adds the line of the client's waits).
- podvm: classify a payload with `Payload::interp` or `memfd::eligible_payload`, not with the head alone.
- podnet: `refused()` works on macOS now.
