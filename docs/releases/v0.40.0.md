# podcrates v0.40.0

The fortieth release of podcrates. For podnet: the poll, quic, udp, dns
and icmp carriers of `pod-link` (PodUtils/podnet#515, #521) and the gate
of row E2-1 (T-520). For podrunner and githome: `cpu_s` and
`peak_rss_mib` in the run reports (T-527, PodUtils/podrunner#86). For
podssh: the forms of the public file hosts (T-528, T-529,
PodUtils/podssh#230) and the tests of `pod-netstack` that it asked for
(T-531, PodUtils/podssh#231).

## How a consumer takes it

Each podcrates dependency names the tag `v0.40.0`.

- podnet: set `[profile.dev.package.kcp] overflow-checks = false` in your
  root `Cargo.toml` with this take (#139, T-526): the clock of KCP wraps
  by design. Set `window: MAX_WINDOW` with `?kcp-nodelay=1`: at 20 % drop
  the factor is 3.7 to 5.3 times the clean time, against 9 to 17 times
  with the window of 256 (call C284). A struct literal of `Facts` takes
  `cost`.
- podssh: read the facts `on-by-default` and `fetch-link-prefix` of each
  file host (PodUtils/podssh#230). Take the tests of T-531 for your T-771.
- podrunner: fill `cpu_s` and `peak_rss_mib` in your reports
  (PodUtils/podrunner#86).
- podvm: move the tag.

## Breaking

- `pod-link`: `Facts` has the field `cost`. A struct literal of `Facts`
  names it, or starts from `Facts::STREAM`.
- `pod-job`: `RunReport` and `PublicReport` have the fields `cpu_s` and
  `peak_rss_mib`. A struct literal names them (`None` for unknown).

## Known defect

- `pod-link`: an `arq-timeout` ends the whole link, not one stream
  (T-514, open); the ARQ has no congestion window (T-515, open); the
  bytes of a poll listener outside `max_bytes` (T-525, open); the fix of
  `kcp` upstream (T-526, partial).
- The test bounds of `pod-link` under an emulator are scaled since
  T-535; the next nightly proves riscv64 and armv7.
- `pod-relay`: the client of the HTTP road sends no `POST close` when the
  app drops its stream (T-478, open).
- `pod-netstack`: a stream dropped before its handshake ends finishes the
  handshake later (T-482, open); the reactor gives one ACK for the
  segments of one poll (T-197, open).
- `pod-session`: a random failure of one test under load (T-204, open).
- `pod-gsr` on Windows ARM64: `STATUS_ACCESS_VIOLATION` (T-131, open).
