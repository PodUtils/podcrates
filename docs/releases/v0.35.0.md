# podcrates v0.35.0

The thirty-fifth release of podcrates. For podnet: the rest of
`pod-warp` but its adoption trials (T-336 to T-339, T-406, with T-393 and
T-395 of the stack), PodUtils/podnet#449. For podssh: the kind
`file-host` and a board of retained messages over MQTT (T-416, T-417,
PodUtils/podssh#220). For each consumer: `pod-oci` against CNCF
distribution and `pod-turn` against coturn on the loopback (T-353, T-380,
T-358).

## How a consumer takes it

Each podcrates dependency names the tag `v0.35.0`.

- podnet: `pod-warp` for its WARP chain: the map of the README of the
  crate ("The adoption map"). The adoption trial of your `src/warp/` is
  T-412 (in work); the freeze of `src/warp/` follows when the trial
  passes. The port rules of PodUtils/podnet#518 are T-475.
- podssh: `pod-services` (`file-host`) and `pod_wormhole::mqtt::Board` for
  its T-738; `pod-turn` and the STUN client are proven against coturn.
  The trial of `pod-warp` in your `crates/podssh-warp` is T-413.
- podvm: move the tag (`pod-oci`, `pod-netstack`).
- podrunner: move the tag when its session starts again with podvm.

## Breaking

- `pod-netstack`: a socket that the TCP timeout ends reads `TimedOut`,
  not `ConnectionReset`.
- `pod-warp`: `DataPathCheck::servers` is a list of `Resolver`, and
  `Table::decide` takes a `rules::Lookup` (T-338).
- `pod-wormhole`: `Packet::Publish` has a field `retain`.
- `pod-oci`: a registry over plain HTTP that asks for a login gets none:
  the client refuses with `Class::Policy`.

## Known defect

- `pod-netstack`: the reactor gives one ACK for the segments of one poll
  (T-197, open).
- `pod-session`: a random failure of one test under load (T-204, open).
- `pod-gsr` on Windows ARM64: `STATUS_ACCESS_VIOLATION` (T-131, open).

## Publish

`publish.yml` runs on the tag: the dry run of `cargo publish --workspace`.
The switch `crates-io` of `Cargo.toml` is false (the owner, 2026-11-09).
`pod-link`, `pod-turn`, `pod-devtunnel`, `pod-warp` and `pod-netlab` are
not reserved on crates.io yet: the owner reserves them before a publish.
