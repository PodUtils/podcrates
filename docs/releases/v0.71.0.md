# podcrates v0.71.0

The seventy-first release of podcrates. It carries the asks of podrunner
first (the ruling "podrunner first"): the ref of a checkout of another
repository, the time zone of `on.schedule` in a new crate `pod-time`, two
more forms of `FileLock`, the fields of `pod-job` that githome and
podrunner need, and the state of an incus instance for podvm's tier of
podrunner. It also carries the asks of podssh and podnet of
2026-11-17 and 2026-11-18, and the fixes of the red nightly run.

## Tests and checks

- `pod-time`: `tests/zone.rs` (no daylight time, the table then the footer, a gap, a repeat, a zone of the south, a table with no footer, the names, a directory, each prefix and each changed byte of a file, `Europe/Berlin` of the host). The fuzz target `tzif_zone` with three zones of tzdata as seeds.
- `pod-cloudflare`: `a_struct_list_whose_size_overflows_is_an_error` (it panicked on `i686` before the fix), the seed `struct_list_overflow`; the tests of the stand-in and of `edge_h2` build and run on Windows (T-915).
- The bounds of a hang of `pod-iroh`, `pod-relay`, `pod-egress` and `pod-guest` scale under an emulator; three races of the tests of `pod-relay`, `pod-ws` and `pod-session` are gone (T-916).
- `pod-job`: seven new examples; the schemas hold each new rule but the bytes of `log_tail`.
- New plants: `job-unknown-reason-of-a-count`, `cloudflare-capnp-list-size-wraps`, `time-gap-keeps-the-offset-of-the-instant`, `core-lock-probe-makes-the-file`, `incus-oom-metric-name-by-prefix`, `relay-policy-cause-dropped`, and those of T-905 to T-913.

## How a consumer takes it

Each podcrates dependency names the tag `v0.71.0`; `issue-sync.yml@v0.71.0` takes `podcrates_ref: v0.71.0`.

- podrunner: take `pod-time` in place of `podrunner-local/src/cron.rs` (`crates/pod-time/README.md`, "Adoption map"); `Cron::parse` gives `pod_time::Error`. Take `FileLock::lock_until` for the wait of a group of `concurrency:`, and `try_lock_existing` and the read-only open for the lock of a runner directory; drop the last `libc::flock` calls. A checkout of another repository takes an empty, a full or a commit ref.
- podrunner and githome: the new optional fields of `job-offer.v1` and `run-report.v1` (`crates/pod-job/README.md`, "Versions of a record"); the sandbox is the field `sandbox`, not `payload` (call C783).
- podvm: `Client::instance_state`, `Client::profile` (copy the `root` device of the default profile and add `size`) and `Client::oom_kills`.
- podssh: the policy line keeps the relay's cause; put the check of `port 25 is refused` back. The drain, the race, the restart and the TLS cause of PodUtils/podssh#314 and #315.
- podnet: `pod_testkit::refused::refused()` in place of your `dead_port` helpers; `podlab run --expect-exit` and `--expect-stderr` for a row of a failure.
