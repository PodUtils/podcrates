# podcrates v0.20.0

The twentieth release of podcrates. For podnet: `pod-netstack`, the
userspace TCP and UDP stack of its virtual network, on smoltcp 0.14, with
the policy, the gateway service and the recorder as traits of the
consumer. For podssh: the host key of `localhost-run`, the service
`sandhole` and the Bare v3 node `openbare-xyz` in `pod-services`.

## Crates

| Crate | Change | Consumers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `pod-netstack` | new: the stack of podnet's `podnet-vnet` as PodUtils/podnet#427 cut it (`stack.rs`, `stack/reactor.rs`, `service.rs`, `socket.rs`, `datagrams.rs`, `device.rs`, `Subnet`) from podnet `f7ed86b`; `NetStack`, `TcpStream`, `TcpListener`, `UdpSocket`, `UdpDatagrams`, the devices; the hooks `Admit`, `GatewayService` and `PacketTap`; smoltcp 0.14. Fixed against the source: a receive with no room in the channel to the app lost its bytes, and a refused listen, connect or bind kept its socket (T-023; call C53) | podnet (`adoption/podnet/pod-netstack.patch`); `pod-warp` later |
| `pod-services` | `localhost-run` has `host-key-sha256` (T-185); `sandhole`, the demo of Sandhole with SSH on 22 and on 443 (T-186); `openbare-xyz`, kind `bare-node`, a TompHTTP Bare v3 node (T-187) | podssh (PodUtils/podssh@T-565, PodUtils/podssh@T-743, PodUtils/podssh@T-740) |

## How a consumer takes it

Each podcrates dependency names the tag `v0.20.0`. A binary that links
`pod-netstack` sets `SMOLTCP_IFACE_MAX_ADDR_COUNT = "8"` in the `[env]` of
its `.cargo/config.toml` (the README of the crate). podnet applies
`adoption/podnet/pod-netstack.patch` (its trial passes at podnet
`f7ed86b`): `podnet-vnet` takes the crate, its `Acl`, its resolver and its
`Recorder` implement the three traits, and each smoltcp of podnet moves to
0.14.

## Breaking

- None for a crate of an earlier tag. `pod-netstack` is new.

## Known defect

- `pod-netstack`: Reno and CUBIC of smoltcp 0.14 grow the window by one
  segment for each ACK; on a link that gives one ACK for each round trip
  they are slow (T-188, open). The default (no controller) is not.
- `pod-gsr` on Windows ARM64: a test process ends with
  `STATUS_ACCESS_VIOLATION` (T-131, open).

## Publish

`publish.yml` runs on the tag: the dry run of `cargo publish --workspace`.
The switch `crates-io` of `Cargo.toml` is false (the owner, 2026-11-09).
