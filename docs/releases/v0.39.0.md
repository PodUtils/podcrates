# podcrates v0.39.0

The thirty-ninth release of podcrates. For podnet: the fix of its live
WARP throughput step (T-508, PodUtils/podnet#524), the rules of its
WebSocket carrier (T-506), the selective-repeat ARQ of `pod-link`
(T-310) and a fuzz target of the record opener of `pod-e2e` (T-510).

## Crates

| Crate | Change | Consumers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `pod-warp` | the datagrams of a session (`WarpNet::datagrams`) send an IPv4-mapped destination as IPv4 and give the answers of that peer back in the mapped form, so a QUIC endpoint on them (it binds `[::]`) reaches an IPv4 peer; before, the packet left as IPv6 to `::ffff:a.b.c.d`, which WARP's network drops; `nc::udp_via_net` takes an answer of its target in either form (T-508) | podnet, podssh |
| `pod-link` | `ws::Ws::with_protocol` (the subprotocol, for example `podnet/1`); a text message ends the link with Close 1003; a Ping each 20 s and an end when no frame comes in 10 s (`Ws::beat`) (T-506, calls C259, C260); the module `arq`: a selective-repeat ARQ (`Arq`, `ArqStream`, `ArqHandle`) over any `datagram::Datagrams`, so a `Link` runs over a lossy datagram carrier; frames `DATA`, `FIN`, `ACK`, `PROBE`; at most 256 frames in flight; RTO of RFC 6298; a rekey before the sequence wraps (T-310, calls C262 to C265) | podnet |
| `pod-ws` | `Serve::protocol`: the accept loop asks for a subprotocol (426 with the name, and the 101 names it); `server::{switching_to, offers, check_protocol, is_protocol_name}`, `handshake::check_chosen_protocol`, `close::code::UNSUPPORTED` (T-506) | podnet, podssh |
| `pod-e2e` | `fuzzing::records`, an exporter of fixed keys for the fuzz target `e2e_record` (T-510) | none |

## How a consumer takes it

Each podcrates dependency names the tag `v0.39.0`.

- podnet: drop your own fixes of `src/warp/quic.rs` and of nc (your
  T-332, `one_family.rs`): the datagrams of `WarpNet` take a mapped
  destination, and `udp_via_net` takes a mapped answer. Your WebSocket
  carrier sets `with_protocol("podnet/1")` at the dial and
  `Serve::protocol` at the bind; your lossy carriers run under
  `pod_link::arq`.
- podssh, podvm, podrunner: move the tag.

## Breaking

- None.

## Known defect

- `pod-link`: an `arq-timeout` ends the whole link, not one stream
  (T-514, open); the ARQ has no congestion window (T-515, open).
- `pod-relay`: the client of the HTTP road sends no `POST close` when the
  app drops its stream (T-478, open).
- `pod-netstack`: a stream dropped before its handshake ends finishes the
  handshake later (T-482, open); the reactor gives one ACK for the
  segments of one poll (T-197, open).
- `pod-session`: a random failure of one test under load (T-204, open).
- `pod-gsr` on Windows ARM64: `STATUS_ACCESS_VIOLATION` (T-131, open).

## Publish

`publish.yml` runs on the tag: the dry run of `cargo publish --workspace`.
The switch `crates-io` of `Cargo.toml` is false (the owner, 2026-11-09).
`pod-link`, `pod-turn`, `pod-devtunnel`, `pod-warp` and `pod-netlab` are
not reserved on crates.io yet: the owner reserves them before a publish.
